#1859. Francisco Conceicao clarifies Juventus bench incident in Vigo

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Francisco Conceicao has denied reports of a tantrum or argument with Juventus staff after his substitution in the Bianconeri’s 2-1 Europa League comeback win at Celta Vigo.

The winger started in Spain, where Hugo Alvarez put Celta ahead before Nick Woltemade equalised. Reports described an angry reaction from Conceicao, including a claim that he struck the bench and went to the dressing room after speaking with a member of Luciano Spalletti’s staff. Conceicao’s account was different: he said he did not argue or speak with a staff member, and had gone inside briefly to change his shirt and use the bathroom before returning.

He acknowledged hitting the area of the bench where he sits, but rejected the suggestion that this formed part of an altercation. The clarification was reported by Football Italia and Corriere della Sera. Randal Kolo Muani then scored in stoppage time to complete Juventus’ comeback.

Juventus had trailed at half-time, and Spalletti said his team had been late to the ball, challenges and runs. Football Italia reported that the coach also suggested Conceicao had reason to be frustrated with his own performance, while saying his staff denied any altercation.

The result kept Juventus’ Europa League record perfect after two matches. Conceicao said the team had responded strongly after the break, playing the way they do in training, and needed to carry that performance into their next games. His record of goals and assists for Juventus remains a focus as he looks to contribute more in the final third.

The winger identified Lazio as the next test, saying Juventus know their opponents are in good shape. He added that the Bianconeri want to climb the Serie A table and take three points from the fixture.

For now, the episode is defined by the distinction between the reports and Conceicao’s own explanation: he acknowledged striking the bench area but denied a dispute with staff. The immediate focus shifts to Lazio, with Juventus seeking to build on the second-half response in Vigo.

And now, something rather exciting could be on the horizon.

Harvey Elliott is finally getting back to his best while on loan with Valencia

Harvey Elliott's career since Jurgen Klopp left Liverpool has been in a bit of a downward spiral.

Arne Slot allowed the Englishman to stay during the 2024-25 season when the Reds won the Premier League title but, the season after, he was sent out on loan to Aston Villa after a rather impressive Euros for England's Under-21s.

This loan move was expected to get his career back on track but, just a couple of games in, Unai Emery and Aston Villa changed their mind about signing him permanently which meant that the midfielder only played nine games across the entire campaign.

He may have netted Villa's first goal during that time but, the season of not playing massively affected his performance levels.

After this debacle, most Liverpool fans thought Iraola would give Elliott a chance to prove his worth but, once again, he was shipped out.

Admittedly, things were a tad rocky for him at Valencia to begin with as the Spanish media even called him overweight.

However, thanks to a recent update from their manager, Javier Aguirre, it finally feels like the Englishman is proving his worth and is getting back to his best.

"I'm fortunate to be able to communicate in his language," he said in quotes relayed by Super Deporte.

"He looks sharper; he's working with a personal nutritionist. I'm delighted with his attitude.

"He has a lot of quality and is a Valencia CF player . We have to get the best out of him."

Harvey Elliott could reignite his career if he continues performing to these levels with Valencia

However, for one reason or another, as soon as the German exited, things just didn't work out for the midfielder.

Indeed, the fact that Florian Wirtz and other attackers were signed probably didn't help his confidence much, especially since he played so often for Klopp.

But, now that he's got on the good side of Aguirre, he could easily reinvent himself and get his career back on track.

Of course, all the players that stopped him from featuring in Liverpool's first-team squad are still at the club but, if he can dig deep and start scoring and assisting more, he could easily displace his teammates from the starting XI.

He's still only 23 years so, even though it may feel like he's been around forever, Elliott still has an awful lot of his career left to go.

At the moment, it's anyone's guess what could happen but, things certainly seem to be going in the right direction for the "exceptional" midfielder at the moment.
