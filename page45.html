Tottenham Hotspur will be without at least six players for Saturday’s Premier League London derby against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge.

Rodrigo Bentancur is serving the second of a three-match suspension and is joined on the sidelines by injured quintet Mykhaylo Mudryk (ankle), Wilson Odobert, Xavi Simons, Lucas Bergvall (all knee) and Savio (muscle).

Destiny Udogie (knee) missed Spurs’ 3-1 win over Coventry City on Monday, but he could be fit to return this weekend, while Pedro Porro (hamstring), Omar Marmoush, Tosin Adarabioyo (both calf) and Andrew Robertson (unspecified) will also have minor issues assessed ahead of kickoff.

Head coach Roberto De Zerbi will weigh up whether to stick with a three-at-the-back system and match Chelsea’s formation. If so, Mickey van de Ven, Jan Paul van Hecke and Marcos Senesi could be the centre-back trio to start.

Archie Gray began in his favoured midfield position last time out, but he could revert to a makeshift right-back role if De Zerbi switches to a four-man defence and if Porro is not fit to start.

Former Chelsea man Conor Gallagher will be hoping to force his way into the first XI and could link up with Sandro Tonali and James Maddison in centre-midfield, the latter of whom scored on his long-awaited return from injury against Coventry.

Dejan Kulusevski is also back after more than 500 days on the sidelines, but a start in attack is considered unlikely for the Swedish playmaker. Mohammed Kudus is more likely to get the nod on the right flank, while Marmoush or Mathys Tel could begin on the left.

Dominic Solanke, who like Gallagher began his career at Chelsea, has scored just one goal in seven Premier League games this term, but the striker is still expected to lead the line once again this weekend.

#2779. Christian Ilzer criticises ‘artificial’ UEFA Nations League

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Hoffenheim head coach Christian Ilzer has criticised UEFA’s scheduling and the upcoming UEFA Nations League, which has resulted in an increase of injuries.

“UEFA’s schedule is insane,” he said after Hoffenheim’s 3-0 UEFA Europa League victory over Lyon. He is referring to injuries sustained within the Paris Saint-Germain and Bayern Munich squads in the UEFA Champions League.

Notably, Bayern’s Luis Díaz will be sidelined for four to six weeks after suffering a thigh injury against Arsenal.

Ilzer noted that clubs try to make adjustments to their daily routines, but with a game every three to four days, muscular injuries are to be expected, and with the FIFA World Cup in the summer, the majority of players have had no time off to recover from an already packed schedule.

As noted by Kicker, Hoffenheim released 18 players for international for the September-October international break. And with another international break on the horizon, there’s little respite for players.

“The focus for national teams should be the European Championship and the World Cup,” explained Ilzer. “These are the major highlights for the players, the major highlights for all national associations.

“That’s what you should be working towards, that’s what you have to qualify for.

“You don’t need an artificial Nations League. In my opinion, the Nations League is simply too much for what the schedule can handle.”

La Lazio e il Gruppo Pastorello, che cura gli interessi di Romano Floriani Mussolini, avrebbero programmato un incontro per ratificare un rinnovo fino al 2030. L’appuntamento potrebbe tenersi la prossima settimana. L’intesa non è ancora stata firmata e prevederebbe anche un ingaggio quadruplicato rispetto agli attuali circa 150 mila euro a stagione. Secondo il Corriere dello Sport, i colloqui sono arrivati a questo punto dopo un avvio di stagione in cui il classe 2003 ha trovato spazio nella squadra di Gennaro Gattuso.

Il passaggio atteso è l’incontro tra la società biancoceleste e i rappresentanti del giocatore. L’obiettivo sarebbe ratificare l’accordo per prolungare il contratto fino al 2030, ma la firma resta da apporre. Anche l’adeguamento economico è, per ora, una previsione legata al nuovo contratto: Floriani percepirebbe circa 150 mila euro all’anno e la cifra dovrebbe essere quadruplicata con il rinnovo.

La prospettiva del nuovo accordo arriva mentre Floriani si sta ritagliando un ruolo in prima squadra. È una parentesi importante nel suo cammino: al rientro nella Lazio, Floriani riconquista spazio e prosegue la crescita nel settore giovanile. Il percorso che oggi potrebbe condurlo al rinnovo comprende dunque anche quel momento di difficoltà, oltre agli anni trascorsi nelle giovanili.

Accanto al calcio porta avanti gli studi. #2774. Gladbah earn first points with comeback win at underwhelming Stuttgart

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Borussia Mönchengladbach avoided becoming the first team to start a Bundesliga season with seven defeats as they beat Champions League participants Stuttgart away from home in a five-goal thriller.

Sebastian Hoeneß started Dženan Pejčinović and Ermedin Demirović together for the first time since the defeat in Hoffenheim, and that decision bore fruit inside the first five minutes. The Germany U21 international provided the assist for the quickfire volley by the makeshift captain.

Moritz Nicolas should've done much better in Gladbach's goal as he had the chance to parry the ball. With Deniz Undav starting on the bench, the combination of the two strikers created another decent scoring opportunity ten minutes later.

However, the visitors started to dominate proceedings from the half-hour mark. Shuto Machino and Fabio Chiarodia failed to test Dennis Seimen from promising positions before Joe Scally stunned Stuttgart with his second goal from as many away games.

On his home debut, the young goalkeeper was unable to react fast as the US defender guided Zento Uno's effort into the net with a clever backheel. Gladbach lost all of their six league games since the start of the season, but they should've taken a lead before the half-time break.

Seimen denied Florian Neuhaus before the the woodwork came to the rescue of the hosts from Wael Mohya's attempt in the last couple of actions in injury-time. The trend continued after the break as Mohya wrestled Leo Sauer and Lorenz Assignon before finding the unmarked Machino, who put Gladbach ahead for the first time with a powerful hit.

Hoeneß wasted no time in making triple changes as Undav, Bilal El Khannouss and Jamie Leweling joined Stuttgart's attack. The hosts tried to respond quickly, but the cellar-dwellers doubled their advantage after yet another scramble around Stuttgart's box.

The magnificent Mahoya scored for the second week running with a close range header from Scally's pinpoint delivery. Seimen was at fault as he was unable to deal with Mahoya's weak effort that led to a second chance for the 17-year-old midfielder.

Stuttgart continued to push to get back in the game, but they were unable to beat Nicolas until Demirović's header halved the deficit in the 83rd minute. The Bosnian striker’s second strike of the night was the 22nd goal Gladbach conceded this season.

After his mistake that led to the opening goal of the game, Nicolas had a strong second-half performance, including his 90th minute save to deny El Khannouss. Alexander Blessin celebrated the huge win with his players after they weathered several crosses into the box in injury-time.

Gladbach will host Karlsruhe in the midweek cup action, while Stuttgart travel to Paderborn for the second time this month.

#2772. Napoli vs Roma - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Jonathan O'Shea | 22 Oct 2026 18:52, Last updated: 22 Oct 2026 18:55

Both winners in midweek, southern giants Napoli and Roma will meet at Stadio Maradona on Saturday evening, to play out this season's first Derby del Sole.

While they scored four goals apiece in Tuesday's Champions League games, the hosts trail Roma by seven points in Serie A after some dismal domestic results.

Having struggled to match expectations, the pressure was piled high on Napoli earlier this week, as they sought to recover from chastening back-to-back defeats.

Losing to Villarreal in the Champions League was one matter, but being beaten by pointless Venezia caused quite a stir in Naples and beyond.

Kevin De Bruyne had missed a penalty as the Partenopei were upset by Serie A's rock-bottom side, so there was a great sense of relief when they won a seven-goal thriller against Bodo/Glimt.

Though Napoli twice allowed Bodo to pull level at the Maradona, they ultimately came out on top, with Scott McTominay's brace helping them over the line.

Even after posting the club's 100th victory in Europe, Max Allegri is still struggling to win over the fans, with his team only sitting ninth in Italy's top flight.

Trying to take the positives from a difficult time, they have kept clean sheets in their last two Serie A home matches - and recent history will be squarely behind them on Saturday.

As hosts, Napoli are unbeaten in eight league games against Roma, which represents their best run in this fixture since the 1980s.

However, current form suggests the visitors could be ready to end their long drought at the Maradona, having made a fine start to the campaign.

Roma have already picked up 17 points in Serie A - their best tally after seven games since 2014 - following a 2-0 victory over Genoa last weekend.

Santiago Castro's clever improvised finish broke the deadlock, before Emanuele Lulli scored his first senior goal, leaving the capital club tucked behind league leaders Inter and city rivals Lazio.

Then, they bounced back from a dramatic defeat to Real Madrid - in which they had led with eight minutes remaining but somehow ended up empty-handed - by thumping Slovan Bratislava 4-0 in the Champions League.

Top scorer Donyell Malen calmly converted a penalty as the Giallorossi strolled to victory at Stadio Olimpico, moving them onto four points from three games so far.

While they still face a challenge to make the knockout phase - notably, double champions Paris Saint-Germain are their next opponents - Gian Piero Gasperini's team must quickly shift focus back to Serie A.

Boasting Italy's top defence, and yet to concede an away goal this term, Roma will now seek their first win in Naples for more than eight years.

After finally getting off the mark in Europe, Napoli may keep changes to a minimum, though Stanislav Lobotka, Sam Beukema and Benoit Badiashile will all hope to win a recall this weekend.

Allegri is still without Alessandro Buongiorno, David Neres, Costantino Favasuli and Alisson Santos due to injury, but Antonio Vergara has just resumed team training and could be involved.

Set to start up front, Rasmus Hojlund has previously played 223 minutes against Roma without scoring a Serie A goal, and he has only struck twice for the Partenopei this season.

Meanwhile, Malen has netted nine in as many club games so far; his preferred partner will be missing, though, with Paulo Dybala still sidelined by a calf strain.

Roma's midfield fulcrum and first-choice goalkeeper are also unavailable: Manu Kone (thigh) and Mile Svilar (toe) have been ruled out.

Lorenzo Pellegrini added to Gasperini's woes by picking up a new injury in midweek, but Bryan Cristante is now back in full training.

#2771. Bundesliga Player Ratings  | VfB Stuttgart 2-3 Borussia Mönchengladbach

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Borussia Mönchengladbach have managed to avoid setting the record for the worst-ever start in Bundesliga history, collecting their first points of the season and winning their first match under new head coach Alexander Blessin with a 2-3 away victory over VfB Stuttgart. The Bundesliga’s last-placed team remain rock bottom of the table, but have finally become the last top-flight team to pick up points in the 2026/27 campaign.

Stuttgart, meanwhile, have now lost five of their seven league fixtures and could even potentially be pulled into the relegation zone before the weekend is out. Despite a promising start to their Champions League cycle, Sebastian Hoeneß’ Swabians have collected just six domestic league points in the young season.

Hoeneß made six changes to the XI that delivered an encouraging performance in the Champions League midweek. One enforced injury change saw Lorenz Assignon deputize for the unavailable Josha Vagnoman. In addition to the right-back switch, Finn Jeltsch, Atakan Karazor, Leo Sauer, Ermedin Demirovic, and Dzenan Pejcinovic started in place of Jeff Chabot, Grischa Prömel, Bilal El Khannouss, Deniz Undav, and Jamie Leweling. Sauer – a not inexpensive summer addition – made his starting debut for his new Bundesliga club.

Blessin, dealing with both injury problems and the threat of an unwanted Bundesliga record, made two changes to the starting XI that lost last Sunday against Hoffenheim. As expected, Shuto Machino worked in place of the injured Isac Lidberg up front. Defender Kevin Diks also had to withdraw at short notice due to personal reasons. This meant that Daiki Hashioka joined his Japanese compatriot Machino as a new BMG starter. Hashioka slid into Blessin’s back three while Philipp Sander took over Diks’ central position.

After Stuttgart attempted to breach the penalty area via the short passing game in the opening few minutes, Ermedin Demirovic suddenly opened the scoring with what initially appeared a highly speculative distance effort. The VfB forward uncorked a strong effort from about 22 meters out in a central position in the 5th minute. Gladbach keeper Moritz Nicolas managed to take the zip off Demirovic’s shot with a sprawling paw, but the ball rolled off Nicolas’ palms and into the back of the net anyway.

The visiting Gladbach players nearly produced an immediate response via a nice individual run from Wael Mohya into the box in the 7th minute. Unfortunately, Mohya’s teammate Hugo Bolin – despite having plenty of space – sent a close-range effort wide.

From that point forward, the Swabian hosts looked more likely to double their advantage. Pejcinovic (14th) and Assignon (16th) generated more efforts on goal. Ramon Hendriks also nearly executed a neat turn-and-finish off a corner in the 18th minute.

Gladbach completely lost the thread for a time, unable to press off the ball or advance forward when in possession. Matters were truly looking dour for the Bundesliga’s last-placed team as they did nothing but chip desperate long vertical balls forward.

Shortly after the half-hour mark, a nice bit of dribbling from Bolin on the left at least produced a little excitement in front of the Stuttgart net. Bolin sent a square ball across the goalmouth. VfB keeper Seimen would have been beaten, but Machino simply couldn’t catch up to Bolin’s layoff.

Not long after that, the Foals were gifted an excellent chance off a dead ball. Angelo Stiller fouled Bolin outside the area in the 34th minute. After an injury delay (Stiller himself caught an elbow), Blessin’s charges unveiled an intricate set-piece variation that looked well rehearsed.

A flick-on from Florian Neuhaus found Fabio Chiarodia, but the defender couldn’t strike the ball from a sufficiently threatening angle, and Seimen collected easily.

A couple of minutes after the dead ball, Stuttgart were caught off guard. With a little bit of luck, Gladbach dug out the equalizer in the 38th minute. The highly touted Mohya was once again involved with a strong carry up the left. Lukas Ullrich tagged along and sent a cross into the box that Maximilian Mittelstädt could only clear as far as Zento Uno. Joe Scally then directed Uno’s shot on goal into the net with a filthy, slick backheel.

Scally’s equalizer completely changed the tenor of the match. Taking advantage of five minutes of added time tacked on due to Stiller’s injury break, the Foals came very close to snatching the lead before the break.

Neuhaus forced a full-stretch parry out of Seimen at 45+4. Seconds before it was time to head into the tunnel, Mohya received a double chance at 45+5. The young BMG phenom’s second effort struck the crossbar. Uno turned the rebound just wide. The Swabians were extremely lucky the score still stood 1-1 at halftime.

After the restart, it didn’t take long for Gladbach’s 17-year-old Wunderkind to pick up where he left off. Mohya drove an unforgettable sequence in the 51st minute. Dribbling near the touchline and double-teamed by Sauer and Assignon, Mohya broke past both his markers and recovered from a slight slip to emerge with the ball and deliver an inch-perfect cutback for his teammate Machino.

Gladbach’s makeshift lead striker finished deftly, but the 2-1 goal that put the guests ahead owed everything to the exceptional hard work of his young colleague. Mohya couldn’t help but (perhaps inadvertently) take a bow in front of the VfB Kurve, a move which was not appreciated by the Swabian ultras. Mohya received whistles amid several more brilliant touches.

One certainly had the feeling that Mohya wasn’t done for the evening. Hoeneß, meanwhile, reacted to the surprise deficit by employing a triple substitution and changing up his tactics. Three fresh attackers – Undav, El Khannouss, and Jamie Leweling – entered in the 55th minute.

The Stuttgart scorer of the stunning distance opener, ironically enough, couldn’t find the right finishing touch when gifted better opportunities closer to the net. Demirovic produced a tame effort in the 58th minute and shot wide in the 63rd. At the other end, Mohya would eventually score his own goal in the 66th minute on a play that – while somewhat slapstick – did exemplify his team’s work ethic on the evening.

Seimen couldn’t quite handle Mohya’s low shot, but it appeared as if the ball wouldn’t go in thanks to Hendriks clearing it off the line. Scally entered into a full-force scramble with Seimen and Mittelstädt for the rebound. Somehow, the USMNT international emerged the winner from this scrum and sent the ball back across the face of goal for Mohya.

The BMG teenage talent then headed the ball across the line after all.

With 20 minutes remaining, Blessin opted to get both Scally and Mohya off the pitch. Gladbach’s amazing turnaround owed much to top-notch performances from those two players. Summer acquisition Yukhym Konoplia – whose injury contributed to Scally remaining with Gladbach –  made his club debut.

Rather poor marking from the freshly inserted Konoplia did finally allow Demirovic to score his second goal of the evening in the 83rd minute. There was some late excitement as the match drew to a close. Hoeneß’ men nevertheless didn’t come particularly close to snatching a draw. Substitute El Khannouss furnished the best chance at 90+1, but Nicolas easily proved equal to the Moroccan international’s effort.

The 17-year-old produced a sensational display that proved decisive in Gladbach’s first Bundesliga victory of the season. Mohya’s dribbling and directness troubled Stuttgart throughout the evening. His outstanding individual work to set up Shuto Machino’s 51st-minute goal – beating two defenders, recovering from a slip, and delivering a perfect cutback – stood out as the match’s finest attacking sequence. Mohya then demonstrated his determination by following up his own effort to head home Gladbach’s third.

The teenager’s emergence has been particularly exciting to follow. His recent promotion to Germany’s U21 squad provided another indication of his growing reputation, while his appearance in the decisive European Championship qualifier against Georgia offered further valuable international experience.

An honorable mention must go to Joe Scally (8). The American international’s ingenious backheel equalizer transformed the match, and his determination to rescue a seemingly lost ball also created Mohya’s decisive third goal. Scally was outstanding, but Mohya’s relentless attacking threat and match-winning contributions give the youngster the narrow edge.

Inter che vuole proseguire il proprio percorso positivo in Serie A, reduce da un avvio con sei vittorie e un pareggio in sette partite, con un ottimo rendimento casalingo. Di fronte ci sarà la Fiorentina di Vanoli, che arriva dalla sconfitta per 1-3 subita al Franchi contro il Como, lo stesso punteggio con cui la squadra di Chivu ha conquistato tre punti nella trasferta di Bologna dell'ultimo turno di Serie A.

Sono 177 i precedenti tra Inter e Fiorentina in Serie A: i nerazzurri guidano il bilancio con 74 vittorie, contro le 46 dei viola, mentre 56 pareggi completano il quadro. L'Inter ha realizzato complessivamente 275 gol, contro i 224 della Fiorentina. Nelle ultime 18 sfide di campionato contro la squadra toscana, l'Inter è rimasta imbattuta in 16 occasioni, conquistando 10 vittorie e sei pareggi. Nelle sei gare più recenti, i nerazzurri hanno ottenuto quattro successi, un pareggio e una sconfitta.

La Fiorentina ha perso tutte le ultime tre trasferte di campionato contro l'Inter, dopo essere rimasta imbattuta in tre delle quattro precedenti, con una vittoria e due pareggi. Per ritrovare una serie più lunga di sconfitte esterne consecutive contro i nerazzurri in Serie A bisogna risalire al periodo compreso tra il 2001 e il 2013, quando i viola incassarono 12 ko di fila. La sfida in programma sarà inoltre appena la seconda tra Inter e Fiorentina in Serie A disputata alle 12:30. L'unico precedente in questa fascia oraria risale al 22 aprile 2012, quando le due squadre pareggiarono 0-0 allo stadio Artemio Franchi.

L'Inter si presenta alla sfida con i viola al primo posto in classifica al pari della Lazio: dopo sette giornate i nerazzurri hanno il miglior attacco del campionato (21 gol segnati, con una media di tre a partita) e nove subiti. Solo cinque volte nella storia della Serie A una squadra è rimasta senza sconfitte nelle prime otto giornate stagionali incassando più reti: la Lucchese nel 1936/37 (10), la Roma nel 1955/56 (10), il Milan nel 1992/93 e nel 1999/00 (11 in entrambe le occasioni) e l'Inter nel 2004/05 (13). La squadra di Cristian Chivu è imbattuta da 17 partite di Serie A, con 11 vittorie e sei pareggi: si tratta della striscia più lunga senza sconfitte nella gestione dell'allenatore romeno. In generale, l'Inter non registrava una serie più lunga in campionato da settembre 2023-aprile 2024, quando arrivò a 28 gare consecutive senza ko.

I nerazzurri hanno vinto tutte le quattro partite casalinghe disputate in questo campionato, segnando 15 gol. Negli ultimi 50 anni, soltanto Roma, Juventus e Sampdoria sono riuscite a vincere ciascuna delle prime cinque gare interne stagionali in Serie A realizzando più reti: la Roma nel 1990/91 (16) e nel 2016/17 (17), la Juventus nel 2014/15 (17) e la Sampdoria nel 2017/18 (16). L'Inter è inoltre una delle squadre che hanno conquistato più punti in rimonta nelle prime sette giornate stagionali nell'era dei tre punti a vittoria: 10, come la Juventus nel 2013/14 e la stessa Inter nel 2021/22. Carattere e voglia di vincere, capaci di reagire davanti alle difficoltà e ribaltare le partite trovando la via della rete in più modi con diversi protagonisti: la squadra di Chivu è una delle due, insieme alla Lazio, ad aver già mandato a segno 10 giocatori diversi in questa Serie A.

Nell'era dei tre punti a vittoria, solamente quattro centrocampisti hanno realizzato più reti contro una singola avversaria nella competizione: Cristiano Doni contro l'Inter, Andrea Pirlo contro il Parma e Clarence Seedorf contro la Juventus, tutti a quota otto, e Marek Hamšík contro Palermo (otto) e Bologna (nove). Çalhanoglu è anche tra i giocatori più efficienti sotto porta in questo campionato. Tra quelli con più di una rete all'attivo, il turco ha una media di un gol ogni 69,7 minuti giocati. Solamente Ange-Yoan Bonny (64,5), Donyell Malen (66,3) e Sandro Kulenovic (55,0) presentano un rapporto migliore.

Marcus Thuram sta vivendo un grande momento a livello realizzativo: Tikus è infatti andato a segno nelle ultime sei presenze casalinghe in Serie A. Nell'era dei tre punti a vittoria, solamente due giocatori sono arrivati ad almeno sette partite interne consecutive a segno con l'Inter: Romelu Lukaku nel 2021 e Christian Vieri nel 2003, autore di una serie arrivata a 11 gare. Il francese è inoltre tra i giocatori con più di una rete in questo campionato con il miglior rapporto tra minuti giocati e gol segnati: una rete ogni 64,5 minuti, al pari di Ange-Yoan Bonny tra i quattro migliori marcatori per rendimento realizzativo.

La Fiorentina è stata l'avversaria contro cui Marcus Thuram ha segnato il suo primo gol in Serie A con l'Inter. Il francese è andato a segno con un bel colpo di testa su cross di Dimarco nella vittoria per 4-0 del 3 settembre 2023 a San Siro, partita in cui ha fornito anche un assist a Lautaro nella ripresa. Contro i viola è arrivato anche il primo gol in Serie A di Petar Sucic con la maglia nerazzurra. Il centrocampista croato ha segnato il 29 ottobre 2025, nel successo interno per 3-0: una magia arrivata dopo un meraviglioso dribbling con la suola. Anche la prima rete in Serie A di Henrikh Mkhitaryan con l'Inter è arrivata contro la Fiorentina, nel successo esterno per 3-4 del 22 ottobre 2022: una rete decisiva, arrivata nell'ultimo minuto di recupero di un match rocambolesco e spettacolare.
