#2460. Rennes vs Strasbourg - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Joel Lefevre | 23 Oct 2026 01:14

Rennes will hope to remain unbeaten at home in Ligue 1 this season when they welcome Strasbourg to Roazhon Park on Sunday.

As things stand, the Brittany side are sixth in the table after a 4-3 loss to Paris FC last weekend, with Strasbourg falling to 14th following their 3-1 defeat versus Paris Saint-Germain.

After a solid start to October, winning their first two Ligue 1 affairs, Stade Rennais will enter this weekend on a two-match winless run across all competitions.

Franck Haise’s men have conceded a combined six goals in their last two competitive games, squandering a 2-1 lead versus Juventus on Thursday in the Europa League in a match which ended in a 2-2 draw.

Rennes have dropped points in two of their three fixtures in the League Phase of that tournament but remain in a position to make the knockout stage, sitting 13th with five points.

While they have given up multiple goals in four of their five competitive away matches this season, this team are on a four-match winning run across all competitions at home, conceding a goal or fewer in those last three outings.

While the Brittany club boast the highest goals per match rate in Ligue 1 (2.1), they have also given up the second-most goals among teams currently in the top half of the table (14).

Les Rennais can extend their top flight winning run versus Strasbourg to four matches this weekend, going unbeaten in six straight meetings against them at Roazhon Park.

As the saying goes, it is not how you start but how you finish, a phrase Strasbourg can relate to all too well over the past few weeks.

Hugo Oliveira’s men have drawn first blood in three of their last four league outings but  failed to win each time, dropping eight points as a result.

That has them only three points above the automatic relegation line heading into this weekend, with those eight points dropped the difference between 14th and first.

Another defeat for them on Sunday would signal their longest losing run in this competition (four matches) since losing four in a row in 2024-25.

Strasbourg have allowed the second-most goals in Ligue 1 thus far (15), 10 of which have occurred away from home.

While they have not beaten Rennes at Roazhon Park since 2018 (4-1), Le Racing boast a 100% record in Brittany this year, winning at Lorient and Brest a season ago.

Last season’s top goalscorer in Ligue 1, Esteban Lepaul, could be forced out of this clash due to an ankle injury, as he was sidelined in their Europa League clash against Juventus this week.

That would be a huge blow as the top goalscorer in the league had a hat-trick in a losing effort versus Paris FC, while Musa Al-Taamari and Boulaye Dia netted in their midweek draw with Juventus.

On the Strasbourg side, Sebastian Nanasi and Ismael Doukoure could be sidelined through injuries, as could Martial Godo with a muscle strain, while Maxi Oyedele and Deivid Washington have knocks.

Hermann Malonga and Fabio Balde are questionable because of injuries as well, while Giovanni Reyna had the only goal for them against PSG last week.

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Southampton midfielder Finn Azaz has issued West Ham with a warning ahead of Saturday’s Championship showdown at the London Stadium. The Saints midfielder has described the fixture as “massive” and suggested it provides his side with an opportunity to “stamp our authority” on the division.

Azaz also insists Southampton believe they are capable of beating anybody, and that confidence is understandable. As we have highlighted in our Claret & Hugh Promotion Watch, the Saints’ league position is somewhat misleading because of the four-point deduction imposed at the beginning of the campaign.

Without that penalty, Southampton would be only one point behind West Ham in the table. So whilst Nuno Espírito Santo’s team have made significant progress in recent weeks, Saturday’s opponents have every reason to believe they can challenge the Hammers.

Speaking to the Daily Echo, Azaz acknowledged that West Ham possess arguably the strongest squad in the Championship. However, Southampton clearly view Saturday’s game as an opportunity rather than something to fear.

I don’t doubt for a moment that the Saints believe they can beat anybody. However, if they are serious about making that points deduction irrelevant, these are precisely the games they need to win.

The same applies to West Ham. Victory would put some valuable daylight between the two clubs and strengthen the Hammers’ position among the promotion contenders. As I pointed out in our latest promotion analysis, West Ham’s recent victories have significantly improved their points projection for the season.

Southampton’s formidable defensive record deserves respect, but West Ham enter the weekend as the Championship’s highest scorers. The Hammers have scored 30 goals in their opening 11 league matches, making this a meeting between a team that has become extremely difficult to break down and the most productive attack in the division.

It is difficult to know how much psychological advantage West Ham will gain from their 4-1 Carabao Cup victory at Southampton in August.

Both teams are likely to field significantly different line-ups this weekend, and a league encounter at the London Stadium will present a very different challenge.

Nevertheless, that emphatic result should provide Nuno Espírito Santo with a useful insight into how Southampton might set up and where they can be exposed. The performance of West Ham’s youngsters in that cup victory also demonstrated that the Saints can be troubled by an energetic, attacking approach.

Azaz’s challenge adds a little spice to an already fascinating game but realistically…what else is he going to say? Southampton want to make a statement, but West Ham have an equally significant opportunity to remind their promotion rivals why they are setting the Championship pace.

In Arsenal injury news, it looks as though Arsenal’s injury list is getting shorter. However, it’s not happening quickly enough to relieve Mikel Arteta’s selection headaches before Sunday’s trip to Nottingham Forest.

At his pre-match press conference, the Arsenal boss said Martin Zubimendi has been given a real chance of facing Forest after an ankle problem. Piero Hincapie is not expected to make the trip.

Both players picked up injuries during a training session at the Emirates. The session took place after last weekend’s 2-1 comeback win over Leeds. It was designed for players who had not played enough minutes.

Neither featured in the midweek Champions League win over Lille. Mikel Arteta described the injuries as “far from ideal” and “very unfortunate”.

Zubimendi has since recovered, showing that he is in much better form on the training field on Friday, said Arteta. He will be evaluated following a further session on Saturday ahead of a decision on his availability.

Hincapie is set to take longer to return to full fitness. The Ecuador defender is “evolving really well”, said his manager, but should require another week or two, he predicts.

There are two key long-term absentees. Christos Tzolis picked up a muscle injury on international duty with Greece. However, a new MRI scan has shown he is making good progress and is looking to return within weeks, rather than months, Arteta believes.

William Saliba remains the big concern. He’s still out of contention after his World Cup-winning campaign with France ended prematurely due to a back injury. He is continuing to recover away from the first-team squad. Another scan is expected next week, with the manager describing every scan so far as positive.

The club has tried to take the picture on three occasions. Each time it has been cancelled at the eleventh hour. The commercial obligations meant that the photo had to happen without him. Arteta wants another opportunity to take it when all are present.

If the news is not all good, the fixture is still an optimistic one for Arsenal. The Gunners head into the game leading the all-time series, with four wins and two draws in the last six meetings, the latest in January being a 0-0 draw at the City Ground. Forest have been poor this season. Languishing in the Championship’s lower half, while Arsenal lie second in the table behind Manchester City.

Further encouragement comes in attack, where Bukayo Saka has recovered from illness to come on as a substitute against Lille and score and set up a goal. Arteta added that Arsenal’s attack has been flowing well.

Arteta is already thinking ahead to a possible Champions League clash with Bayern Munich but has stressed that Forest come first. Arsenal need to stay in touch with the leaders, and having Zubimendi back would add to their midfield options for a demanding run of fixtures.
