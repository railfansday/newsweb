#2860. England’s Dutch coach Sarina Wiegman extends contract until 2029

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

See more DutchNews articles in your Google search results

See more DutchNews articles in your Google search results

Dutch football coach Sarina Wiegman has extended her contract with the England women’s team by two years, taking her up to the 2029 European Championships.

Wiegman, 56, led the team to two European titles in 2022 and 2025, as well as their first World Cup final in 2023, when they lost to Spain.

Prior to that she won the European Championships with the Dutch national team in 2017 and reached the World Cup final two years later, losing 2-0 to the United States.

“I am so happy to be able to carry on with this squad and cannot think of a better job in football,” she said in a statement released by the English Football Association (FA).

“Working for a world-class organisation like the FA, and alongside so many people dedicated to winning England teams and the growth of our game, excites me every day.”

FA chief executive Mark Bullingham described Wiegman as “an incredible coach with an unparalleled record in major tournaments”. He added: “There is no one better to lead the Lionesses into the next two tournaments.”

Commentators had widely expected the Dutchwoman to leave her post after next year’s World Cup, after England failed to qualify automatically for the tournament.

A 4-0 loss to Spain in June, the team’s heaviest defeat since Wiegman took charge, means they will have to come through a play-off tournament with Greece, Ukraine and Slovakia to reach the finals in Brazil.

Wiegman said: “Anyone who knows me will understand that my main concern right now is absolutely the World Cup, and helping this squad realise their potential in the years to come.

“We have an important job to do to confirm our place in Brazil, and hopefully we can do that and give the country another special summer to remember next year.”

By Carter White | 22 Oct 2026 16:08

Aiming to pick themselves off the bottom of the Championship rankings, Preston North End welcome West Bromwich Albion to Deepdale for an all-important clash on Saturday afternoon.

The Lilywhites have continued to struggle in the second tier despite the recent arrival of Graham Alexander to the managerial hotseat, whilst the Baggies are back on track following consecutive wins.

Anyone expecting a new-manager bounce at Deepdale would have been bitterly disappointed since the return of club football after the international break, with fans' favourite Alexander losing three straight games at the beginning of his Preston homecoming.

Following a narrow Lancashire loss to Millwall on October 10, the Lilywhites have shipped a staggering eight goals across two insipid away defeats, with Birmingham City (4-0) and Wrexham (4-1) taking advantage of a side lacking even a drop of confidence.

Currently in the midst of a six-game winless run (D1 L5) dating back to the start of September, Preston have sunk to rock bottom in the Championship standings with just a single victory and a mere four points from 11 contests.

The Lilywhites simultaneously possess the poorest attack and weakest defence based on goals scored (9) and conceded (24) in the division so far, with Alexander's side netting on just four occasions across five home league matches in 2026-27.

Two of those strikes arrived during Preston's only Championship triumph of the campaign to date on September 5, when former Wigan Athletic star Callum Lang bagged a brace in a 2-1 success over Lancashire rivals Blackburn Rovers.

Whilst a dark cloud circles Deepdale, there was jubilation at The Hawthorns last Saturday afternoon, when West Bromwich Albion came from two goals behind to secure a last-gasp win over Tony Mowbray's Blackburn.

Netting the 89th-minute clincher, summer signing Felix Horn Myhre was the hero off the bench for the Baggies, with the Norwegian midfielder producing his maiden moment of magic for Albion following a mixed start to life in English football.

Barney Stewart and Issac Price also breached the goal of Blackburn in the 3-2 victory, with the latter up to four second-tier goals for the season, leading Aune Heggebo (3) and Jimmy-Jay Morgan (3) in West Brom's Golden Boot race.

Most Albion supporters were expecting midtable mediocrity at best during James Morrison's first full season in charge, however, Saturday's visitors are deservedly in fifth spot after 11 matches, five points behind pace-setters Swansea City.

Following comeback successes over Wrexham and Blackburn, the Baggies are on a mission to secure three consecutive Championship victories for the first time since a four-match winning run under Carlos Corberan in August/September 2024.

Preston's injury problems have failed to ease up amid a troubling run of form, with Harry Clarke (hamstring) and Liam Gibbs (muscle) recently added to the treatment list.

The pair join long-term Lilywhites absentee Ali McCann, who has not featured since the EFL Cup defeat by Everton in late August.

More positively, American Caleb Wiley featured in a training match on Tuesday, with the 21-year-old nearing a return to first-team action.

West Brom Director of Medical Tony Strudwick has confirmed that midfielder Toby Collyer is likely to be sidelined until December with a hamstring injury picked up during the Blackburn win.

The former Manchester United youngster's absence places more emphasis on Rabby Nzingoula, Ousmane Diakite and Jayson Molumby performing in the middle of the park.

Brayann Pereira has seemingly earned the trust of head coach Morrison in the absence of Noah Galves (knee), with the right-back starting both of Albion's recent victories.

Impressionnant sous les couleurs du Bayern Munich contre Arsenal en Ligue des champions, Harry Kane a une nouvelle fois marqué les esprits. Ses performances répétées au plus haut niveau continental ont poussé l'ancien international britannique Steven Gerrard à lui rendre un vibrant hommage.

Le mercredi 21 octobre 2026, l'Allianz Arena accueillait une affiche de prestige pour la 3e journée de la phase de ligue de la Ligue des champions. Opposé à Arsenal, le Bayern Munich a concédé un match nul spectaculaire (2-2). Au terme d'un duel européen disputé avec une rare intensité, Harry Kane a d'ailleurs été désigné homme du match.

L'attaquant anglais a offert une passe décisive en profondeur à Alphonso Davies sur l'ouverture du score à la 25e minute. Après l'égalisation londonienne, le capitaine des Three Lions a ensuite converti un penalty à la 61e minute pour donner l'avantage au Bayern Munich (2-1). Avec cette nouvelle réalisation, Harry Kane a marqué lors d'un dixième match consécutif en Ligue des champions, revenant à seulement une longueur du record historique de Cristiano Ronaldo. Une régularité impressionnante au sommet du football européen.

Cette prestation face au club londonien a immédiatement suscité l'admiration des consultants britanniques. Au micro de TNT Sports, Steven Gerrard n'a pas hésité à placer l'attaquant munichois au sommet de l'histoire du football anglais.

L'ancien milieu de terrain emblématique de Liverpool a poursuivi son propos en affirmant : « Car, si l’on songe aux immenses joueurs que nous avons connus, Harry Kane est assurément dans la conversation. » Le joueur de 33 ans affiche aujourd'hui 125 sélections avec l'équipe d'Angleterre, partageant ce record historique avec le gardien Peter Shilton. Ses 91 buts consolident en outre son statut de meilleur buteur de l'histoire des Three Lions. Toutefois, Harry Kane n'a remporté aucun trophée collectif en sélection, malgré un Soulier d'or au Mondial 2018, un titre de co-meilleur buteur à l'Euro 2024, deux finales disputées à l'Euro (2020 et 2024) et une troisième place obtenue à la Coupe du monde 2026. Un bilan paradoxal sur la scène internationale.

Cette reconnaissance individuelle fait également écho aux titres conquis depuis son départ en Allemagne. Après une décennie sans trophée avec Tottenham malgré trois titres de meilleur buteur de Premier League, Harry Kane a enfin garni son palmarès en Bavière. Avec le Bayern Munich, le canonnier a conquis deux championnats d'Allemagne (2025, 2026), une Coupe d'Allemagne (2026) ainsi que deux Supercoupes d'Allemagne (2025, 2026). D'ailleurs, il a également terminé meilleur buteur de la Bundesliga lors des trois dernières saisons. Une consécration tardive mais totale.

L'attaquant anglais a bouclé l'exercice précédent avec un total de 73 buts marqués sous les couleurs de son club et de sa sélection. « Je dirais simplement que la saison dernière a été incroyable, tant sur le plan personnel que collectif. C'était, de loin, la meilleure saison de ma vie », a confié Harry Kane. Désormais favori des bookmakers, il pourrait ainsi devenir le cinquième joueur anglais à remporter le Ballon d'or lors de la cérémonie prévue le lundi 26 octobre 2026. Avant cette échéance, le Bayern Munich se rendra sur la pelouse du SC Fribourg à l'Europa-Park Stadion le dimanche 25 octobre 2026 à 17h30 pour son prochain match de Bundesliga.

Une opportunité majeure pour Karl Sorin, colosse de 1,85 m et 135 kg, qui ne comptait jusqu'alors qu'une seule cape internationale chez les moins de 20 ans, fêtée le 19 mars 2023 face au pays de Galles.

Pourtant, rien ne laissait présager un tel avenir en équipe de France pour Karl Sorin. Plus jeune, le Choletais avait connu des débuts difficiles avec le ballon ovale lors d'une première tentative vers l'âge de 5 ou 6 ans, quittant le terrain en larmes. C'est finalement à 13 ans que l'histoire s'est enclenchée par hasard dans les allées d'un commerce de Cholet. Repéré par un bénévole du club local en raison de sa corpulence, l'adolescent a alors accepté de tester la discipline. Une révélation immédiate.

Le jeune joueur intègre dans la foulée le Rugby Olympique Choletais et évolue d'abord au poste de troisième ligne centre. Grâce à sa puissance naturelle et son activité sur le terrain, il ne tarde pas à attirer l'attention des formateurs de l'élite. Ses prestations pleines d'énergie tapent dans l'œil du Stade Rochelais, qui le fait venir sur la côte Atlantique dès 2018, alors qu'il n'a que 15 ans.

Dès son arrivée en Charente-Maritime, les éducateurs rochelais prennent une décision capitale pour sa progression. Devant son gabarit imposant, ils choisissent de repositionner Karl Sorin comme pilier droit. Il dispute ensuite son tout premier match professionnel en Top 14 le 20 août 2023 à Montpellier sous le maillot jaune et noir, en dépannant comme remplaçant au poste de pilier gauche. Une première apparition pour lancer son parcours au plus haut niveau.

Afin d'accumuler du temps de jeu, il est prêté lors de la saison 2024-2025 à Soyaux-Angoulême en Pro D2. Sous les ordres d'Alexandre Ruiz et aux côtés du pilier d'expérience Yassin Boutemmani, il prend part à dix rencontres disputées pour assimiler les exigences de la mêlée fermée. Un passage déterminant. À l'été 2026, il signe ainsi son premier contrat professionnel avec le Stade Rochelais jusqu'en juin 2027.

Durant la saison 2026-2027, le droitier confirme tous les espoirs placés en lui. Karl Sorin a déjà disputé sept matches de Top 14 avec La Rochelle, comptant cinq titularisations pour un bilan parfait de cinq victoires. Au quotidien, il affine notamment les détails techniques du combat sous les ordres d'Uini Atonio, l'ancien international rochelais désormais entraîneur spécifique de ce secteur clé.

Avant de rejoindre les Bleus, Karl Sorin doit affronter l'Union Bordeaux-Bègles avec La Rochelle le dimanche 25 octobre 2026 à 21h05 au stade Marcel-Deflandre, pour le compte de la 7e journée de Top 14. Le stage des 42 joueurs débutera ensuite le lundi 26 octobre 2026 au Centre national du rugby à Marcoussis, afin de préparer le test contre les Fidji programmé le samedi 7 novembre 2026 à Lyon.

#2841. Andy Woodman leaves Bromley: Who are top five longest-serving managers in Premier League, EFL?

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Darren Plant | 22 Oct 2026 16:19

With only seven clubs in the Premier League and EFL having kept their current managers in the dugout for longer than four years, changes to the longest-serving managers list for those four divisions is a rarity.

However, on Thursday, League One club Bromley announced that Andy Woodman - who was appointed in March 2021 - had left his role by mutual consent.

Having accumulated just eight points from 10 matches to sit in 23rd place in the League One table, it was deemed that the 55-year-old's time at Hayes Lane had run its course.

Here, Sports Mole takes a look at the new top-five list, with Woodman having dropped out from third position.

With Arsenal winning last season's Premier League and earning draws against the likes of Bayern Munich in the Champions League, the Gunners have no plans to depart with Mikel Arteta anytime soon.

Earlier in October, the Spaniard signed a new contract that will run until the end of 2029-30. Barring a desire to take on a new challenge, it is plausible that the 44-year-old could become one of the longest-serving managers in Premier League history.

Arteta, at six years and 304 days, is already 21st in that particular list, and he is motivated to add to a Premier League trophy, FA Cup and three Community Shields.

The former midfielder boasts a record of 223 wins, 68 draws and 74 defeats from 365 matches.

Remarkably, Arteta is out in front by nearly a year, but Nigel Clough closing in on six years at Mansfield Town is an achievement in itself.

Clough is no stranger to long managerial reigns. He has amassed 1,598 matches in charge of just the four clubs, with two spells coming at Burton Albion.

At Mansfield, the 60-year-old delivered promotion from League Two in 2023-24, and has since consolidated with 17th and 10th-placed finishes in League One.

With the Stags having 15 points from 10 games in the third tier in 2026-27, Clough is in no danger of being sacked, but his current contract is due to expire at the end of the season.

There has been recent talk of Phil Parkinson departing his role at Wrexham, a consequence of a relatively slow start to the new Championship campaign.

Nevertheless, the 58-year-old has already secured legendary status at the Racecourse Ground, a consequence of spearheading Wrexham's charge from the National League to the second tier of English football in three consecutive seasons.

A total of 161 wins, 69 draws and just 56 defeats have been posted in 286 matches. As a manager, Parkinson now has six promotions at four different clubs to his name.

However, while he retains the backing of the club's Hollywood owners, he will need to move Wrexham up the Championship table from their current 14th position.

A recent addition to the list through Barnet's 2024-25 National League title success, Dean Brennan moves into fourth place in these rankings.

Brennan has taken a different career path to others in this list. From 2010 to 2025, he worked at a number of non-league clubs, while he was initially head of football at Barnet before taking on the managerial reins when Harry Kewell left in September 2021.

Despite Barnet's return to the EFL, the 46-year-old has still won nearly half of his 265 matches in charge, recording 132 wins, 61 draws and 72 defeats.

With the Bees in second place in League Two with two games in hand, Brennan is targeting taking the club into the third tier of English football for the first time since 1993-94.

Ahead of Leyton Orient's Richie Wellens by just 27 days, Chesterfield boss Paul Cook is the newest member of this top-five list.

After an unsuccessful stint with Ipswich Town, Cook took charge of Chesterfield in February 2022 when they were in the National League. After seventh and third-placed finishes, Cook lifted the title in 2023-24.

Chesterfield have since finished seventh and sixth in League Two, narrowly missing out on promotion to the third tier.

Cook, who is closing in on 1,000 matches as manager, has racked up a record of 121 wins, 59 draws and 72 defeats during his 252-game reign at the SMH Group Stadium.
