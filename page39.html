#2899. Man City guilty verdict sparks UAE investment warning over UK economy

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Naim Beneddra | 22 Oct 2026 15:15, Last updated: 22 Oct 2026 15:24

Manchester City's financial rule breaches could have consequences far beyond the Premier League, with the United Arab Emirates reportedly threatening to reconsider billions of pounds in future UK investment. However, experts have questioned whether such a move would seriously damage the British economy.

The dispute surrounding Manchester City's ownership has taken on a diplomatic dimension after an independent commission found the club guilty of 114 of the 115 charges brought against it for breaches of Premier League financial regulations.

The findings, published on September 29, concern financial arrangements between 2009 and 2018, including the use of commercial agreements that the commission concluded had artificially inflated the club's revenues.

City have appealed the decision, maintaining their innocence and arguing that the commission's conclusions contain significant errors of law and fact. No final sporting sanctions have yet been determined.

According to Bloomberg, Emirati officials have warned the British government that the Premier League's actions could affect relations between the two countries, potentially jeopardising future investments.

The Telegraph subsequently reported that funding for a major technology development project between Oxford and Cambridge could be among the investments at risk.

The warnings have raised questions about the extent to which a disciplinary case involving a football club could influence Britain's economic relationship with one of its most important Gulf partners.

However, international relations and legal experts interviewed by Brazilian publication Trivela have suggested that the potential consequences may be considerably more limited than the figures initially indicate.

Manchester City's connection to the UAE extends directly into the country's political and financial establishment.

The club has been owned since 2008 by Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan, who is also the UAE's vice-president and brother of President Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan.

City chairman Khaldoon Al Mubarak is meanwhile the chief executive of Mubadala Investment Company, one of Abu Dhabi's leading sovereign wealth funds.

Those connections have brought the Premier League's disciplinary proceedings into a much broader diplomatic discussion.

According to Bloomberg, the UAE has invested more than £30bn in the United Kingdom over the past five years, particularly in sectors including defence, renewable energy, technology and life sciences.

The figure illustrates the scale of the economic relationship, although it does not mean that £30bn of existing investment is currently threatened with withdrawal.

Instead, the concerns centre primarily on future commitments, including possible Emirati funding for the Oxford-Cambridge growth corridor.

The project aims to establish a major British technology and innovation hub, with the government seeking substantial private investment to support its development.

However, specialists consulted by Trivela believe the UK's diverse investment base would reduce the wider economic consequences of any Emirati withdrawal.

Rafaella Krasinski, a lawyer specialising in business law, explained that individual projects could suffer without necessarily creating a nationwide financial crisis.

"The UK has diversified sources of capital and does not depend exclusively on the Emirates," Krasinski told Trivela.

"A possible reduction could affect specific projects, but would not necessarily cause a broader economic crisis."

International relations professor Tanguy Baghdadi also emphasised the strength of the existing relationship, arguing that the UAE has established considerable trust with Western countries through decades of economic cooperation.

The significance of a potential withdrawal would therefore depend on which investments were affected and whether alternative sources of funding could be secured.

The situation is further complicated by comments from Mubadala deputy chief executive Waleed Al Muhairi, who indicated on October 13 that the Manchester City case was separate from the fund's investment activities.

That position contrasts with the warnings attributed to other Emirati officials, suggesting that the country's various investment institutions may not necessarily respond in the same way.

The possibility of economic consequences has intensified scrutiny of the British government's relationship with Manchester City's owners.

Prime Minister Andy Burnham, who served as mayor of Greater Manchester between 2017 and 2026, faced criticism after expressing concern about the possibility of the Emirati owners leaving the club.

Burnham highlighted their contribution to Manchester's development, including investment in the Etihad Stadium, the surrounding area and other projects across the city.

His comments prompted concerns among rival Premier League clubs that political considerations could influence the disciplinary process.

Downing Street subsequently insisted that the independent proceedings must be respected and that those responsible for any proven wrongdoing should face the appropriate consequences.

Meanwhile, Bloomberg revealed that Al Mubarak met Business Secretary Jonathan Reynolds in Downing Street on September 14, shortly before the commission's findings became public.

British government sources maintained that the meeting concerned investment and bilateral economic relations, rather than Manchester City's financial case.

Nevertheless, Baghdadi believes the government's desire to preserve relations with the UAE could create additional political pressure.

"The British government could say: 'If City have to be punished, punish them, but take it slowly. Let's try to find a solution, some alternative,'" he told Trivela.

"I don't think the Premier League would necessarily accept that automatically, but it is another source of pressure coming from the political sphere."

The professor's comments describe a possible scenario rather than evidence that ministers have attempted to interfere with the proceedings.

Ricardo Bruno Boff, another international relations expert interviewed by the Brazilian publication, suggested that public opposition to interference could also discourage the government from becoming involved.

Krasinski similarly warned that any proven political intervention could undermine confidence in the integrity of English football.

The Independent Football Regulator's chairman, David Kogan, has also pledged to resign if political pressure compromises the body's independence.

For Manchester City, the immediate uncertainty remains the outcome of their appeal, which is not expected to be heard before January 2027.

Potential sporting consequences could include a substantial points deduction or relegation, although those punishments remain hypothetical and will depend on the relevant disciplinary decisions.

Despite the off-field uncertainty, City have continued their impressive Premier League form under Enzo Maresca, winning their opening seven league matches this season.

The wider dispute illustrates the unusual position occupied by a club whose ownership is closely connected to a foreign government's political and economic interests.

For Britain, any reduction in Emirati investment could create difficulties for particular projects. For English football, however, the more immediate question is whether the disciplinary process can remain independent of those wider financial considerations.

#2897. Alkmaar moves protest against Israeli fans amid safety concerns

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

See more DutchNews articles in your Google search results

See more DutchNews articles in your Google search results

The mayor of Alkmaar, Anja Schouten, has ordered two demonstrations against Thursday’s Europa League match between AZ and Hapoel Be’er Sheva to be moved away from the stadium because of concerns about potential disorder.

Hundreds of people are expected to attend the protests against the participation of Israeli clubs in European competition, which have been organised by pro-Palestinian groups Boycott 4 Palestine and Week 4 Palestine.

The protests were originally due to take place outside the main entrance to the AFAS Stadium, but Schouten ordered the demonstrators to gather near the Kooimeer roundabout, the main road junction leading to the stadium, instead.

“It is not possible to take adequate management measures to prevent disorder and separate fans going to the stadium from demonstrators,” Schouten said.

“This [new] location allows us to facilitate the safe arrival and departure of demonstrators. We also need to prevent confrontations taking place with other groups.”

A post shared by Week.4PalestineNL (@week.4palestinenl)

In a briefing note to councillors, the mayor said there were indications that supporters of the Israeli club were planning to travel to Alkmaar in spite of the ban.

“There are fears that protesters will come into conflict with each other, with supporters’ groups or with the police, that vandalism could ensue, or that the safety of people attending the match could be endangered,” she wrote.

Schouten came under fire from opposition parties on the city council last week for her decision to ban Israeli supporters from travelling to the match. AZ Alkmaar cancelled ticket sales to visiting fans in the wake of the mayor’s decision.

She said the decision was based on public order concerns rather than “political considerations”, following the riots surrounding a Europa League game between Ajax and Maccabi Tel Aviv in Amsterdam two years ago.

Schouten said it was “virtually impossible” to keep groups apart outside the stadium, especially as many visiting fans were expected to stay in Amsterdam for the game.

Alkmaar had originally said Israeli supporters were welcome at the game, but the mayor changed her mind after her counterpart in Amsterdam, Femke Halsema, made an “urgent” appeal not to allow a repeat of the scenes in the capital in 2024.

VVD councillor John van der Rhee accused Schouten of succumbing to a “powerplay” by the mayor of Amsterdam.

#2896. Dia sulla Lazio di Gattuso: «Non è un mago, semplicemente c'è del lavoro dietro»

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

L’ex biancoceleste difende il tecnico dai giudizi sulle esperienze passate e racconta perché aveva fiducia nel gruppo.

Boulaye Dia continua a seguire la Lazio e non è sorpreso dal suo percorso con Gattuso. L’attaccante senegalese, oggi al Rennes, racconta che lui e i compagni conoscevano le possibilità della squadra già durante la preparazione. Prima della gara di Europa League contro la Juventus, invita anche a non definire in crisi i bianconeri per la sconfitta contro la sua ex squadra.

Dia, intervistato da Diretta.it, guarda i risultati della Lazio e, quando può, anche le partite. A Roma ha lasciato amici ai quali è ancora legato: dice di voler bene a tutti e di essere contento quando i biancocelesti vincono. Con alcuni ex compagni, tra cui Reda Belahyane e Noslin, si sente di tanto in tanto, anche se non ogni giorno.

Il primo posto condiviso con l’Inter non era un risultato che si aspettasse in questi termini. Dia distingue però la posizione in classifica dalla fiducia che aveva nel gruppo. All’arrivo di Gattuso e del suo staff, racconta, lo spirito portato dal tecnico aveva rappresentato un rinnovamento dopo la stagione precedente, anche sul piano mentale. La preparazione estiva gli aveva dato la convinzione che la squadra sarebbe cresciuta.

Nel suo racconto trova spazio anche la sconfitta per 2-0 contro il Mantova in Coppa Italia, che considera una scossa per il gruppo. Per questo, pur non avendo previsto l’attuale posizione della Lazio, Dia non si dice sorpreso dalla crescita della squadra: conosce il contributo dell’allenatore e ricorda in quali condizioni aveva lasciato i compagni.

La fiducia in Gattuso, aggiunge, c’era già in vista della stagione e il lavoro svolto finora rafforza il suo giudizio positivo. Dia respinge l’idea che i precedenti meno felici del tecnico, soprattutto con la Nazionale, bastino a valutarlo. Sottolinea che dietro i risultati c’è lavoro, che ogni club ha un contesto diverso e che, a suo giudizio, Gattuso sta portando avanti molto bene il compito assunto alla Lazio.

Il prossimo avversario del Rennes in Europa League è la Juventus, nella terza giornata della League Phase. Dia non ritiene che la sconfitta dei bianconeri contro la Lazio autorizzi a parlare di crisi. Richiama il valore della sua ex squadra e osserva che, in ogni caso, la situazione della Juventus riguarda il club torinese, non il Rennes.

La formazione francese arriva all’impegno dopo il 4-3 subito in casa del Paris FC. Dia ha seguito dalla panchina buona parte di una gara segnata da continui cambiamenti ed è entrato nel finale. Perdere così, dice, fa male nonostante lo spettacolo offerto dalla partita. Dalla panchina aveva cercato di capire quali situazioni sfruttare al suo ingresso; in campo, invece, gli episodi si sono susseguiti rapidamente, tra un rigore, un fallo, una decisione sul penalty e un gol annullato.

Non è convinto che quella sconfitta possa essere letta automaticamente come una spinta per la sfida europea. In campionato il Paris FC ha sorpassato il Rennes grazie alla vittoria, maturata in una partita che, secondo Dia, la sua squadra avrebbe potuto anche pareggiare o vincere. L’Europa League, però, è un contesto differente.

Ripensando alla sua esperienza biancoceleste, Dia torna sulle indicazioni ricevute da Baroni al suo arrivo. L’allenatore gli aveva spiegato di essere pronto a utilizzare due punte e di sapere che poteva giocare anche alle spalle dell’attaccante. Pur preferendo il 4-3-3, in molte gare della stagione aveva poi schierato Dia e Taty Castellanos insieme in avanti.

Prima della Lazio c’era stata la Salernitana, con una stagione da 16 gol. Dia conserva ricordi molto belli di quella squadra e degli allenatori avuti a Salerno: prima Davide Nicola, poi Paulo Sousa. Dice di essersi trovato particolarmente bene con il gioco di Sousa, adatto alle sue caratteristiche, in un periodo in cui aveva fiducia e si sentiva a proprio agio. Di Nicola apprezza invece l’energia trasmessa dalla panchina e la capacità di spingere i calciatori a dare tutto.

Tra le reti segnate in granata c’è quella contro il Napoli nel 2023, che rinviò la festa scudetto degli avversari. Dia la ridimensiona sul piano personale: il Napoli avrebbe comunque conquistato il titolo pochi giorni dopo e la Salernitana non aveva vinto quella partita. Prima di entrare, vedendo lo stadio già pronto a festeggiare, aveva pensato a quanto sarebbe stato bello segnare e zittire il pubblico. Per i tifosi salernitani, anche per la rivalità, quel gol ebbe un significato speciale.

Al ritorno a Salerno, ricorda, la squadra fu accolta dai sostenitori con fumogeni e festeggiamenti. Dia condivise la loro gioia, ma non colloca quella rete fra le più importanti della propria carriera. Ricorda di aver segnato ai Mondiali e in una semifinale di Champions League. Cita anche il gol decisivo all’ultimo minuto contro l’Hellas Verona, che portò una vittoria alla Salernitana: un risultato diverso dal pareggio ottenuto a Napoli.

#2893. « Pourquoi moi ? » : L'émouvant message de Kvaratskhelia après sa grave blessure au PSG

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Victime d’une rupture du ligament croisé antérieur du genou droit mardi soir lors de la victoire du PSG face au FC Barcelone, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia est sorti du silence ce mercredi 21 octobre 2026 sur son compte Instagram.

Mardi 20 octobre 2026 en Ligue des champions, le PSG a dominé le FC Barcelone (4-2). Mais la fête a été totalement gâchée à l'heure de jeu. À la 60e minute, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia a été contraint de quitter la pelouse après une violente torsion du genou droit consécutive à un contact avec le joueur barcelonais Xavi Espart. Incapable de poser le pied au sol, l'attaquant a dû céder sa place.

Le Paris Saint-Germain a officiellement communiqué la nouvelle ce mercredi matin : son ailier gauche souffre d'une rupture du ligament croisé antérieur du genou droit et devra subir une intervention chirurgicale dans les prochains jours. Face à cette blessure, le joueur de 25 ans a fait part de son désarroi. « Il ne s’est pas écoulé beaucoup de temps, mais une question me trotte dans la tête: pourquoi moi? Je n’ai jamais rien négligé, et je me suis sans doute préparé pour ce match mieux que je ne l’avais jamais fait auparavant. Je pensais être prêt à tout, mais pas à ça », a déclaré Khvicha Kvaratskhelia sur son compte Instagram.

Cette blessure représente un coup d'arrêt pour l'international aux 51 sélections avec l'équipe de Géorgie. L'attaquant parisien avait d'ailleurs été élu meilleur joueur de la précédente édition de la Ligue des champions remportée par le PSG. Mais Khvicha Kvaratskhelia s'est déjà tourné vers sa convalescence.

« Je comprends que Dieu m’a choisi. Et si en cela, j’ai pu protéger d’autres joueurs sur d’autres terrains, je suis prêt à traverser ces mois de travail, de douleur et de larmes. Je sais que je dois revenir plus fort, pour les miens, qui sont toujours à mes côtés », a ainsi détaillé l'ailier parisien. Déterminé à retrouver son meilleur niveau, le joueur a tenu à remercier chaleureusement ses supporters pour leurs nombreux messages d'encouragement : « Je vous le promets: je ne vous trahirai pas, je ne vous ferai pas défaut une seule seconde. Merci à tous pour votre soutien, à très bientôt ».

Cette confrontation continentale contre Barcelone laissera des traces très profondes dans l'effectif parisien. En effet, au-delà de Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, l'infirmerie a accueilli deux autres cadres majeurs au cours de la partie. L'attaquant Ousmane Dembélé a été contraint de sortir dès la 19e minute de jeu en raison d'une lésion à la cuisse gauche, tandis que le capitaine Marquinhos a subi une lésion à la cuisse droite à la 60e minute. Tous deux resteront en soins pendant plusieurs semaines. Un casse-tête pour Luis Enrique.

Ainsi, le Paris Saint-Germain va devoir vite se remobiliser pour négocier un calendrier exigeant sur la scène nationale. Le prochain match du club de la capitale est programmé le dimanche 25 octobre 2026 à 20h45 au Parc des Princes contre l'Olympique Lyonnais, pour le compte de la 8e journée de Ligue 1.

A trip to Bristol City then followed last time out, and the South Yorkshire outfit bounced back to winning ways, seeing out a 1-0 victory having led throughout thanks to Oliver Arblaster's fifth-minute goal.

With clear signs of encouragement in recent displays, and having moved into 1th spot and within two points on the playoffs in a tight mid-table group, Sheffield United will aim to follow that away win up with another three-point haul at Bramall Lane on Saturday.

Their visitors, meanwhile, make the trip to South Yorkshire aiming to stop the rot and reverse their fortunes after a poor start to the season.

After heading into a second full season under John Eustace with optimism on the back of an eighth-placed Championship finish in 2025-26, Derby County have failed to get going thus far, managing just six points from their first 11 matches to sit 23rd.

The Rams have won just one and lost seven of those, with no side scoring fewer than their nine goals and only three conceding fewer than the 20 they have allowed.

Eustace's men last tasted victory at the beginning of September and have since gone winless in seven league outings, most recently hosting Stoke City on the back of a 4-1 loss to QPR and again coming away empty-handed as Luke Graham scored the only goal in a 1-0 away win.

Already sat three points behind 21st spot and five worse off than 20th, Derby County will be desperate to arrest their slump at the weekend and begin a climb up the table.

Sheffield United form (all competitions):

Sheffield United's injury troubles have eased in recent weeks, but they remain without Femi Seriki and Jamie Shackleton.

Hamza Choudhury is back in contention after a suspension, but Wilder may stick with an unchanged back four of Japhet Tanganga, Mark McGuinness, Liam Kitching and Harrison Burrows, who featured after his return from injury.

Patrick Bamford should return and lead the line, while Sydie Peck will hope to regain a midfield spot having been reduced to a substitute appearance last weekend, although Arblaster scored and Kalvin Phillips impressed again at Bristol City in his absence.

Derby County remain badly hit by injuries, with Matt Clarke, Craig Forsyth, Sondre Langas, Charlie Taylor, Eiran Cashin and Patrick Agyemang all sidelined alongside first-choice goalkeeper Jacob Widell Zetterstrom.

Those absences of the back forced the Rams into a makeshift back four of Joe Ward, Ryan Nyambe, Dion Sanderson and Ryan Hedges last time out, although former Blade Jack Robinson has arrived to bolster their options in defence.

Josh Vickers will continue to deputise between the sticks, while Carlton Morris should again lead the line with support from mainstays Bobby Clark and Sammie Szmodics.
