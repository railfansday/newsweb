#2316. How can Man City survive without Erling Haaland? These are Enzo Maresca's four options - from an ideal replica and two false-nine candidates to the academy wild card

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Published: 17:00 BST, 19 October 2026 | Updated: 17:37 BST, 19 October 2026

Erling Haaland is usually striking fear into opponents but it was the turn of Manchester City’s supporters to experience what that feels like at around 5pm on Saturday.

With seconds to play of their tense 3-2 win over Ipswich Town, Haaland set off in an attempt to wrap the game up – and seal his hat-trick – when an innocuous knock left him barely able to walk during an inordinate amount of stoppage time.

Haaland spent the remainder of the afternoon staggering about, at one point down on his haunches, and headed straight down the Etihad tunnel at full time instead of his customary lap of appreciation, which the Norwegian embarks on win, lose or draw.

Enzo Maresca moved to calm any alarm surrounding the striker’s fitness in the immediate aftermath – and there was plenty of that around given Haaland’s 12 City goals so far this season have come at a rate of one every 71 minutes. No team could do without him for any lengthy period of time.

The City boss suggested that Haaland will be assessed ahead of their Champions League tie at home to AEK Athens on Tuesday – a fixture that could have offered an opportunity to rest the 26-year-old anyway.

Despite a summer at the World Cup and general lack of rest, Maresca has started him in every match outside of the Carabao Cup.

Erling Haaland is usually striking fear into opponents but it was the turn of Manchester City’s supporters to experience what that feels like at around 5pm on Saturday

Club chiefs had discussed re-signing Julian Alvarez from Atletico Madrid in the summer but a move did not materialise

So the idea of how City cope without Haaland had been percolating before he pulled up on Saturday. Aware of a lack of genuine No9s, club chiefs had discussed re-signing Julian Alvarez from Atletico Madrid in the summer but a move did not materialise.

And even though Maresca clearly doesn’t view Haaland’s kick on the ankle as serious, what might happen without him is a question not far from the minds of fans. ‘To replace Erling is always difficult,’ Maresca said. The Italian isn’t wrong, although he always seems to have a trick up his sleeve.

Antoine Semenyo would be the favourite to act as the lynchpin of City’s attack in the absence of Haaland. With 13 goals in 37 games since joining from Bournemouth, mostly off the right wing, the Ghana international is the ideal replacement.

His physicality and willing running mean he could take over without City compromising Maresca's style, and the onus would remain on the more attacking players manipulating space in behind for the 26-year-old to roam into.

But Haaland was not the only front-line forward to suffer an injury scare in the final moments of the weekend win, with Semenyo expected to miss the next two months with a knee issue. Semenyo’s problem initially appeared so worrying that City suspected an ACL injury.

‘After the scan we feel much better,’ Maresca said. ‘It is not so big like we expected. Now the most important thing is to recover it. When he is back, probably around December, we have a new player in terms of injury. It's not muscular. We can use Antoine as a nine but also we have different players that we are going to try to find a new solution.’

Antoine Semenyo would have been the favourite to act as the lynchpin of City’s attack in the absence of Haaland - but now he will miss two months with a knee injury

Maresca talks about different solutions, with two options as false nines at his disposal that would have to alter City’s style if called upon. Phil Foden and Rayan Cherki are a couple of possible replacements that could see the tactical outlook seem more akin to a Pep Guardiola team.

Foden has a back catalogue of moments in that position across 2021 and 2022, notably when City beat Liverpool at Anfield and a thrashing of Club Bruges. The academy graduate was brought up on the Guardiola idea of attacking and knits play together in a way that few others can manage, drifting into abnormal pockets and dictating their game.

Cherki would have a fair amount to learn in that role, although is showing a knack for scoring goals having netted five in nine games already this term. Foden is more of a seamless fit into that system, with Cherki instead providing more of an unpredictable presence.

Either way, both of them would have very few problems dragging markers away from their zones to free up space for others – which has so often been the City way.

Phil Foden and Rayan Cherki are false nine options that could see the tactical outlook seem more akin to a Pep Guardiola team

Iliman Ndiaye is more of an outside bet, having rarely been seen in central areas throughout his time in the Premier League. Maresca does think outside the box though and so the £65million summer signing from Everton cannot be discounted.

The 26-year-old possesses the sort of speed and ingenuity in behind to become a real threat and the industry he showed out wide during the win at Old Trafford, when City were a man light, is exactly the sort of energy that can carry a press from the front.

He has experience playing in a central role, just off the striker, at Sheffield United in the Championship, too.

Normally a midfielder, 17-year-old Floyd Samba hasn’t really featured as a central striker in the club’s academy teams but shot to prominence there in the Carabao Cup third round win over Norwich City last month, scoring twice and showing great strength in holding up play.

Floyd Samba (right) scored twice as a striker on his senior debut last month against Norwich

‘When I was younger, like Under 10s, I was mainly a No9,’ said Samba, who switched international allegiances to Germany from England this year and has also represented France's Under 16s.

‘But ever since then, I haven't been really. I have an idea of it because I think last season against Real Madrid, in the Youth League, I played No9 as well. Those little opportunities to make me improve, to play more than one position.’

A plethora of options but Maresca will hope Haaland can stay injury-free and City’s medical staff stay on top of the workload. Even if it is not on Tuesday against AEK, they will need to offer him rest at some point.

#2315. Chris Sutton gives Liverpool v Brighton prediction as he backs 6-month sequence to continue

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Chris Sutton is predicting one unenviable Liverpool sequence to continue when they host Brighton in the Premier League on Sunday.

Incredibly, the fixture will mark six months to the day that the Reds last won a top-flight match at Anfield (3-1 v Crystal Palace), with five subsequent league games in L4 ending in either a draw or a loss dating back to the final month of last season.

This weekend Andoni Iraola’s side come up against a Seagulls outfit who couldn’t be in better form, winning their last four Premier League matches by a combined scoreline of 15-0, including a three-goal thrashing of champions Arsenal.

Liverpool beat Brighton twice at Anfield last term, but Sutton believes the visitors will extend their winning run in the division and extend the Red’s wait for a home win in the league.

In his weekly Premier League predictions piece for BBC Sport, the former Chelsea striker wrote: ‘Liverpool have an extra 48 hours to prepare for this game – they played Villarreal at home on Tuesday, while Brighton are away in the Czech Republic on Thursday.

‘I don’t think it will make any difference, though, because the way that the Seagulls play, I am expecting them to go to Anfield and win.

‘This has the makings of a great game but, while Liverpool have had a couple of good results, I am still not convinced by them. Andoni Iraola’s side are a work in progress with a tendency to empty their midfield and that means Brighton can hurt them.’

Sutton has gone for the Seagulls to come away with a 3-1 victory.

Away wins have been very rare in recent meeting between these two teams – in 11 league and cup clashes since the start of 2022/23, only once has the visiting side emerged triumphant, so at least that’s one good omen for Liverpool.

The Reds have quietly been building momentum under Iraola, winning seven of their last nine games in all competitions, but victory over a red-hot Brighton would probably be their best result of the season so far.

The hosts would have to achieve it without several first-team regulars including Dominik Szoboszlai and Hugo Ekitike. Also, it remains to be seen whether Victor Munoz and Bradley Barcola will be fit to feature, although the latter underwent an individual recovery session on Thursday.

Liverpool’s defence has looked brittle of late, with 10 goals conceded in their last four matches, and the Seagulls are the highest scorers in the Premier League at present with 23 already in this campaign (the Reds have only nine).

Sutton is right about Brighton’s capacity to do damage if the home side aren’t on it from the start, but Iraola’s side have plenty of quality in their own ranks and are good enough to take down any opposition when at their best and end their six-month wait for a top-flight win at Anfield.

#2314. Motherwell vs Hearts - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Ellis Stevens | 23 Oct 2026 11:13

Motherwell will strive to end their five-game losing streak when they welcome Hearts to Fir Park in the Scottish Premiership on Sunday.

The hosts have fallen to 10th in the standings with eight points from nine games, while the visitors are third in the table with 16 points after nine fixtures.

After an encouraging start to his first season as Motherwell manager, Alfred Johansson now finds his position at the helm coming under pressure after a miserable recent run.

The Steelmen remained unbeaten in their opening six games of the season across all competitions, with Motherwell recording four victories and two draws.

However, a 1-0 defeat to Stenhousemuir in the Scottish League Cup second round sparked a considerable downturn in form from Johansson's team, who have now won just one of their last 10 matches.

That includes a disastrous streak of five straight Scottish Premiership defeats before Sunday's fixture, with Motherwell conceding 12 goals and scoring just three times during that run of results.

Consequently, Motherwell have fallen to 10th in the standings with only eight points from nine league games, with their 16 goals conceded only better than St Mirren's (17 conceded) and Kilmarnock's (20 conceded) defensive records.

As the pressure continues building on Johansson, the manager will be desperate to lead his team to a streak-snapping victory on Sunday, although Motherwell will have to overcome a seven-game winless run against Hearts.

The Jambos will, however, head into Sunday's fixture short on confidence following back-to-back defeats, including a particularly humiliating 7-1 demolition against Celtic last weekend.

That loss marked just their second defeat in 13 matches across all competitions, alongside a 2-1 defeat to Falkirk in mid-September, but Wouter Vrancken's side have now suffered two defeats in two matches going into the Scottish Premiership's 10th matchday.

Hearts failed to bounce back during midweek as they lost 2-0 to Trabzonspor, with Mohamed Salah and Franculino Dju ensuring the Turkish side capitalised on their dominance throughout the 90 minutes.

Vrancken will be eager to avoid suffering a third straight defeat when they take on Motherwell on Sunday, especially as defeat could allow Dundee to leapfrog them into third place.

Hearts do travel to Fir Park as the worst performing team in the division away from their home ground, with Vrancken's side accumulating only four points from five fixtures on the road.

The Jambos will, however, look to their prolific attack to lead them to just their second away win of the league season, with only Celtic (23) scoring more than Hearts' 20 goals scored this term.

Motherwell are without the availability of Callum Hendry, Dylan Williams and Oscar Priestman due to injury issues.

Sebastian Palmer-Houlden and Alex Lowry both scored in the eventual 3-2 defeat last weekend, but both should keep their spots in attack on Sunday, with Ibrahim Said and Tawanda Maswanhise also likely to start.

As for Hearts, Ageu, Blair Spittal, Craig Halkett, Tom Renaud, Christian Borchgrevink and Harry Milne are all unavailable due to ongoing injury problems.

Hearts' front four of Josh McPake, Claudio Braga, Calvin Miller and Jamees Wilson have scored 12 goals combined in the league, and all four should start on Sunday.

#2307. Transfer news: Arsenal and Liverpool join race for wonderkid Aleksey Batrakov

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Axel Clody | 23 Oct 2026 11:26, Last updated: 23 Oct 2026 11:27

Five Premier League clubs are reportedly tracking Galatasaray midfielder Aleksey Batrakov after his strong start to life in Turkey, according to TeamTalk.

The 21-year-old was a summer target for Paris Saint-Germain, who never made an offer, and now Arsenal, Liverpool, Manchester United, Chelsea and Manchester City are all said to be admirers.

TeamTalk claims that scouts from Arsenal and Liverpool were present in Lille on Wednesday night to watch Batrakov at close quarters. The Russia international scored, his second goal of the season in the competition, and impressed the representatives of both clubs.

The outlet adds that Arsenal and Liverpool join Manchester United, Chelsea and Manchester City in the race for the attacking midfielder. Those three clubs are also said to be admirers of the Galatasaray player, who are hoping to keep their summer signing for as long as possible.

Batrakov joined Galatasaray from Lokomotiv Moscow in a deal valued at £24m, a significant investment from the Turkish club. The early signs are encouraging, with two goals and two assists in nine matches so far.

According to TeamTalk, Batrakov has a release clause allowing him to join a club in one of Europe’s four major leagues - England, Italy, Spain or Germany - for €50m (£42.5m). That could make a future transfer easier, as the sum is well within the reach of the five English clubs mentioned.

Paris Saint-Germain were keen on the attacking midfielder in the summer, with sporting adviser Luis Campos having confirmed the club were monitoring him. However, the French club did not table an offer and prioritised other deals, renewing their attack with Ferran Torres, Maghnes Akliouche and Mika Godts.

Batrakov did not wait for Paris and chose to move on, and his form in Turkey suggests that decision is paying off.
