Lazio, Monachino sugli infortunati: Cancellieri recuperato, novità su Rovella e Gudmundsson

Arrivano aggiornamenti importanti dall’infermeria della Lazio. Il dottor Pietro Monachino, medico sociale biancoceleste, ha fatto il punto sulle condizioni degli indisponibili, soffermandosi in particolare su Rovella, Marusic, Gudmundsson e Cancellieri.

La notizia più immediata riguarda Cancellieri: il giocatore si è allenato per tutta la settimana con il gruppo ed è stato completamente reintegrato in rosa, tornando così a disposizione di Gattuso. Segnali positivi arrivano anche dagli altri giocatori impegnati nei rispettivi percorsi di recupero.

Secondo quanto riportato da Lazio Style Radio, Monachino ha spiegato che Rovella e Marusic stanno rispettando la tabella stabilita e lavorano già sul campo. Per Gudmundsson, invece, gli ultimi esami hanno fornito risposte positive sulla frattura e sulle parti molli.

Il medico sociale ha iniziato il proprio aggiornamento dalle condizioni di Rovella e Marusic, confermando che entrambi stanno proseguendo secondo il programma concordato con lo staff tecnico.

“Sia Rovella che Adam stanno proseguendo il loro lavoro come avevamo già stabilito. Ci eravamo confrontati e ci confrontiamo di settimana in settimana con lo staff tecnico. Abbiamo una tabella da rispettare e stanno andando assolutamente bene entrambi, quindi siamo molto contenti di quello che stanno facendo. Oggi sono già in campo, prima sono salito con loro, quindi il percorso era quello che volevamo”.

I due calciatori stanno dunque svolgendo lavoro differenziato, ma già sul terreno di gioco.

“Sono assolutamente in campo e quella che era la nostra previsione si sta per il momento consolidando, quindi siamo molto fiduciosi. Abbiamo volutamente preso, grazie anche alla collaborazione dello staff tecnico, i tempi giusti”.

Monachino ha sottolineato come il recupero venga gestito attraverso una progressione attentamente controllata.

“Siamo in una situazione, come ho detto già la scorsa settimana, di progressione, ma una progressione misurata, controllata, che ci sta dando il risultato sperato. Entrambi avevano delle lesioni diverse nella loro modalità, ma delicate per quello che potevano essere i tempi di recupero. E fin qui molto bene”.

La prudenza resta quindi un principio fondamentale nella gestione degli infortuni. Una linea condivisa anche con lo staff di Gattuso.

#2796. Officiel : le match PSG - Lyon reporté !

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

La LFP a officialisé ce vendredi 23 octobre 2026 le report du choc entre le Paris Saint-Germain et l'Olympique Lyonnais au lundi 26 octobre 2026 à 20h30 au Parc des Princes. Cette décision fait suite à un arrêté pris par la Préfecture de Police de Paris.

Ce sommet entre le Paris Saint-Germain et l'Olympique Lyonnais, comptant pour la 8e journée de Ligue 1 McDonald's, était initialement programmé le dimanche 25 octobre 2026 à 20h45 au Parc des Princes.

La décision de reprogrammer la rencontre au lundi 26 octobre à 20h30 a été prise par la LFP en plein accord avec le diffuseur officiel Ligue 1+. D'ailleurs, le déplacement des supporters de l'Olympique Lyonnais au Parc des Princes faisait déjà l'objet d'un arrêté d'interdiction préfectorale avant même ce report officiel. Un casse-tête logistique.

Dans son communiqué officiel publié ce vendredi 23 octobre 2026, l'instance du football professionnel a officialisé cette modification de calendrier : « La LFP prend acte de l'arrêté de la Préfecture de Police (...) et décide, en accord avec Ligue 1+, de décaler le match au lundi 26 octobre à 20h30 », a indiqué la Ligue de football professionnel.

Pour motiver sa demande, la Préfecture de Police a mis en avant la forte mobilisation des forces de l'ordre nécessaire pour encadrer des manifestations contre la vie chère prévues le samedi 24 octobre dans Paris, après de récents débordements. Les effectifs de sécurité sont ainsi sous tension dans la capitale.

En effet, le dispositif sécuritaire francilien du dimanche 25 octobre est également mobilisé par l'organisation d'un match de NFL entre les New Orleans Saints et les Pittsburgh Steelers au Stade de France. Face à ces impératifs majeurs, les pouvoirs publics ont estimé impossible d'assurer la protection du Parc des Princes le même soir.

Au-delà des aspects sécuritaires, ce décalage crée une programmation particulièrement inhabituelle pour le football français. En effet, le nouveau créneau du match fait coïncider le choc de Ligue 1 avec la cérémonie du Ballon d'Or 2026 organisée au London Palladium à Londres le lundi 26 octobre au soir.

La rencontre PSG-OL se tiendra finalement le lundi 26 octobre 2026 à 20h30 au Parc des Princes.

#2793. Chelsea vs Tottenham Hotspur - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Oliver Thomas | 22 Oct 2026 18:00, Last updated: 23 Oct 2026 13:21

Tottenham Hotspur will be looking to build on their first Premier League win of the season when they travel to Stamford Bridge for an intriguing London derby against Chelsea on Saturday evening.

These two teams most recently butted heads just over five months ago when the Blues claimed a 2-1 home victory at the end of last season, courtesy of goals from former midfield duo Enzo Fernandez and Andrey Santos.

Four wins, one draw and two defeats have been posted by Chelsea across their first seven Premier League games, with back-to-back, morale-boosting victories over Bournemouth and Everton helping them climb into the top four of the table.

A thumping 5-1 home win against Bournemouth was followed by a 2-0 away success over Everton last weekend. Pedro Neto scored twice against the Toffees, while Chelsea ended their unwanted 22-game run without a Premier League clean sheet.

The Blues delivered a ‘very professional and serious’ performance on Merseyside in Xabi Alonso’s view. The Spaniard is now keen for his team to build on their recent successes and string together three consecutive top-flight wins for the first time since February.

Only Brighton (23) and leaders Man City (19) have scored more Premier League goals this season than Chelsea (17), who enter Saturday’s contest with Spurs having only lost one of their last 16 top-flight London derbies at Stamford Bridge.

The West Londoners are on the brink of history as they are riding a six-game winning streak in the league against the Lilywhites. A win would eclipse their current record of six consecutive league victories over their rivals, a feat originally achieved between January 2000 and March 2002.

All 20 Premier League teams have now celebrated at least one victory this season after Tottenham ended a miserable six-game winless start to the campaign by beating Coventry City 3-1 in North London, courtesy of goals from James Maddison, Omar Marmoush and Ben Davies.

This much-needed triumph for Roberto De Zerbi's side was just their fourth Premier League victory overall in 2026 (P27 D10 L13). Crucially, it also marked the first time in 36 top-flight matches that Spurs scored more than two goals – a massive relief for a side that narrowly avoided relegation last season.

Sitting 16th in the table but only three points behind the top nine, Spurs have only won back-to-back league games twice since the start of last season and history suggests that they will find it difficult to come out on top at Stamford Bridge this weekend.

Indeed, Tottenham have won on just one of their last 34 visits to Stamford Bridge in the Premier League era (D11 L24) and that was a 3-1 success in the 2017-18 season under Mauricio Pochettino.

Spurs have collected only two points from their last eight visits since then (D2 L6), while they have also lost each of their last six London derbies in the division; they have never lost seven in a row in their history.

Chelsea goalkeeper Emiliano Martinez has undergone surgery on a fractured jaw suffered in the win at Everton and he has now joined Reece James (knee), Jamie Gittens (ankle), Pep Chavarria and Emmanuel Emegha (both unspecified) in the treatment room. Meanwhile, Malo Gusto (illness) is a doubt.

In the absence of Martinez, 21-year-old Mike Penders is set to become Chelsea’s youngest-ever goalkeeper to start a Premier League match, with the 6ft 7in stop-stopper likely to have a three-man defence of Josh Acheampong, Maxence Lacroix and Levi Colwill line up in front of him.

Cole Palmer has contributed to five goals in as many league games against Spurs (three goals, two assists), while Joao Pedro has netted three goals in three top-flight meetings with the Lilywhites. Both players are set to join Morgan Rogers in a fearsome front three.

As for Tottenham, Rodrigo Bentancur will serve the second of a three-match ban and remains out along with injured quintet Mykhaylo Mudryk (ankle), Xavi Simons, Wilson Odobert, Lucas Bergvall (all knee) and Savio (muscle).

Destiny Udogie (knee) missed the win over Coventry and a late call is set to be made on his availability for Saturday. Pedro Porro (hamstring), Omar Marmoush, Tosin Adarabioyo (both calf) and Andrew Robertson (unspecified) are also minor doubts.

Former Chelsea midfielder Conor Gallagher will be pushing for a recall and could play alongside Sandro Tonali and Maddison, while Dejan Kulusevski is back after more than 500 day on the sidelines. However, Mohammed Kudus is set to be preferred from the start on the right flank.
