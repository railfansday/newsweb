Che tra l’altro andrebbero ad alleggerire ulteriormente il monte ingaggi. Una certezza resta McTominay. Per ora nessun passo decisivo ma le sue caratteristiche sarebbero ideali per arricchire un settore destinato a cambiare molto. Al Napoli piacerebbe tanto anche Perrone ma qui non ci sono margini di manovra visto che il Como è pronto a blindarlo.

Tra campionato e Coppa Italia ha già realizzato 9 gol in 8 partite ufficiali. Una media molto buona che ha subito suscitato l’attenzione di tante società. Non esiste recompra ma solo la possibilità da parte del club portoghese di pareggiare un’eventuale offerta. Il Benfica si è assicurato il 20% sulla plusvalenza di una futura rivendita. Varela è una punta completa ed è per questo che anche in Premier League hanno cominciato a muoversi, l’Arsenal su tutti. Insomma il Napoli ci sta pensando anche se sa bene che non sarà così semplice nel caso convincere il Monza ad aprire ad una trattativa già nella sessione invernale. Detto questo resta sempre da capire cosa accadrà con Lang. In estate gli azzurri hanno preso in considerazione l’ipotesi di una sua cessione ma non è mai arrivata una proposta all’altezza. The capital outfit have picked up five points from their last three league matches, beating Espanyol before drawing back-to-back fixtures against Osasuna and Getafe.

Benat San Jose's side are two points above 18th-placed Elche and only two points behind Alaves in seventh, so there could be a lot of movement in this upcoming gameweek.

Rayo have only managed to win 13 of their previous 47 matches against Elche in all competitions, but they did record a 1-0 victory when the pair last locked horns in April 2026.

The Red Sashes finished eighth in La Liga last season, one point behind seventh-placed Getafe, and they will be targeting another push for the European spots this term.

Elche, meanwhile, ended last term in 15th spot in the division, only one point above the relegation zone, so there is plenty of room for improvement during the current campaign.

The Green-Striped Ones have a record of one win, four draws and four defeats from their nine league matches this season, with seven points leaving them in 18th spot.

Martin Anselmi's side have also picked up five points from their last three matches, beating Espanyol before the September/October international break, and then drawing with Celta Vigo and Villarreal.

Elche have won three of their last five matches against Rayo, and they comfortably lead the overall head-to-head record between the two sides, having posted 28 victories to the capital outfit's 13.

Rayo will definitely be without the services of Luiz Felipe and Augusto Batalla for Saturday's La Liga contest through injury, while Andrei Ratiu and Randy Nteka are viewed as doubts at this stage.

Sergio Camello has been in excellent form for Rayo this season, scoring seven times in eight appearances, and the 25-year-old will continue in the final third of the field.

Meanwhile, there should also be starts for both Alvaro Garcia and Isi Palzon.

As for Elche, Marc Aguado will miss the contest due to an ankle injury.

Fer Nino has scored five times in nine appearances this season, and the 25-year-old will lead the line with support from Thomas Lemar in the number 10 position.

Uzun joined Frankfurt in the summer of 2024 and has made 65 appearances across all competitions, scoring 19 goals.

The Turkish winger has netted twice in five Bundesliga matches so far this season and has drawn comparisons with Dimitar Berbatov following a series of classy performances.

Premier League clubs have been reportedly monitoring Uzun for some time, but Arsenal appear to be stepping up their interest in the youngster.

Likewise, Borussia Dortmund, AC Milan and Napoli are also interested in Uzun, who reportedly has a £50m (€60m) release clause.

Uzun is under contract with the German club until 2029, putting Frankfurt in a strong negotiating position as they have little incentive to part ways with such a promising talent on the cheap.

Although the Gunners could look to strengthen their wide attacking options, it seems unlikely that they will invest heavily in a player who is yet to establish himself at the highest level.

The report claims that Arsenal admire Uzun, but waiting another year to assess his development could be a sensible approach before deciding whether to pursue a deal.

The youngster is reportedly not pushing for an exit, although he could find it difficult to resist the temptation of a move if Arsenal make an attractive offer.
