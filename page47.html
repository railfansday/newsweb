The Red Devils have made their worst start to a league season in 40 years and sit 13th in the Premier League table with seven points on the board.

Man Utd suffered their heaviest European defeat since 1994 when they lost 4-1 to Como in the Champions League in midweek, further casting doubt over Carrick's future.

Carrick was handed a two-year contract in May, but the club could decide to part ways with their former player if results fail to improve.

According to a report from the Daily Mail, the Red Devils are admirers of Brighton & Hove Albion boss Fabian Hurzeler, although luring him away from the Amex Stadium could prove difficult.

Hurzeler signed a new contract in June, meaning Man Utd may need to pay a hefty fee to bring him to Old Trafford.

Julian Nagelsmann and Antonio Conte are both currently available, with the former Germany national team boss reportedly thought to be interested in the job.

Likewise, Eddie Howe and Kieran McKenna are also available if Man Utd opt for British managers, although the club's problems run deeper than the identity of the man in the dugout.

Following Ferguson's retirement, few could have imagined that Man Utd would go on to change managers so frequently, with stability remaining elusive at Old Trafford.

The likes of David Moyes, Louis van Gaal, Jose Mourinho, Ole Gunnar Solskjaer and Ruben Amorim have all encountered problems at the club, with each of their tenures eventually coming to an abrupt end.

Carrick is expected to be in the dugout for this weekend's clash against AFC Bournemouth, but there is a growing sense that his time at Old Trafford could be running out.

The performances have fallen well below an acceptable standard, while increasingly vocal dissent within the dressing room is making an already difficult situation even more challenging for Carrick.

Racing have a record of two wins, two draws and five defeats from their nine league matches this season, and eight points has left them 17th in the division heading into the next set of fixtures.

The Green and Whites are the reigning Segunda Division champions, and their main aim for this term will be to consolidate back at this level of football.

Jose Alberto Lopez's side will enter this match off the back of a 2-0 defeat to Osasuna, with that result following a 2-2 draw with Valencia on October 11.

Racing have not been victorious since posting a 2-1 win over Alaves on September 12, collecting only one point from their last four games at this level.

The Green and Whites recorded a 2-0 win over Espanyol when the pair last met in February 2024.

This will actually be the first La Liga meeting between the two sides since March 2012, when Espanyol recorded a 3-1 victory, and three of their last four contests have been won by the Catalan side.

Espanyol have a record of two wins, two draws and five defeats from their nine league matches this season, with eight points leaving them in 16th spot in the division.

Manolo Gonzalez's side will enter this match off the back of a 1-0 home defeat to Atletico Madrid, and they have only picked up one point from their last four games.

Espanyol have not been victorious in Spain's top flight since a 2-0 victory at Osasuna on September 12.

Both teams will turn their attention to the Copa del Rey after this match, with Espanyol taking on Sestao, while Racing will face Ribadesella.

Racing need to make a check on Simon Eriksson, who has a shoulder injury, but the home side are otherwise in strong shape for their clash with Espanyol.

Yassir Zabiri has been in strong form this season, scoring seven times in nine appearances, and the 21-year-old will again feature in the final third of the field.

The experienced Sergio Canales, meanwhile, is set to continue as the number 10.

Espanyol will be missing Kike Garcia through injury, while a late check needs to be made on Jofre Carreras before a final decision can be made.

Roberto Fernandez has been in strong form this season, scoring six times in nine appearances, and the 24-year-old will feature in the final third of the field.

Meanwhile, there should be a start in a forward area for Bryan Zaragoza.
