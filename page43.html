The Bluebirds have seen their fortunes take a turn for the better following a winless start to the campaign, whereas Blues have hit new promotion-chasing heights since the September/October international break.

Following a seven-game winless run (D4 L3) at the beginning of the 2026-27 campaign, Cardiff City have lost one of their past four matches, collecting a respectable seven points as they prove their worth as a competitive Championship outfit.

The Bluebirds appeared set to secure their maiden away victory of the season at the Riverside Stadium last time out; however, second-half goals from Tommy Conway and Will Lankshear salvaged a point for promotion favourites Middlesbrough.

Bagging Cardiff's opener on Teesside last weekend, Jack Moylan has made a seamless step up to the Championship stage this term, with the former Lincoln City playmaker involved in nine second-tier goals already, scoring three times in his past two appearances.

After a valuable-yet-frustrating point at Middlesbrough, Brian Barry-Murphy's men are back at the Cardiff City Stadium, where the Bluebirds are unbeaten in five home league matches (W2 D3), last tasting league defeat at the venue to Wycombe Wanderers in March.

Indeed, Cardiff have enjoyed back-to-back wins on home soil since the middle of September, hitting three past Charlton Athletic before a Moylan brace headlined a 5-1 battering of crisis-hit Watford on October 13.

The only team in the Championship with a 100% record since the end of the September/October international break, Birmingham City are the talk of the town at the moment in respect to the second-tier promotion race.

Disgruntlement over a supposedly lacklustre summer transfer window is now a distant memory after a five-match unbeaten run (W4 D1) and three consecutive Championship wins for the first time since the days of John Eustace in August 2023.

Bluenoses clamoured for youngster Ibrahim Osman to return for a full season at St Andrew's @ Knighthead Park, but the sensational form of Liam Millar has showcased the effectiveness of Birmingham's business in the market.

The Hull City loanee netted a brace in the 3-0 victory over Queens Park Rangers last time out, with the Canada international proving an instant hit in the Second City, providing seven goal contributions in as many matches so far.

Sitting in fourth position in the Championship rankings on 20 points from 11 matches, Blues now return to the Cardiff City Stadium for the first time since December 2023, when Wayne Rooney picked up one of his two victories as Birmingham boss.

After deploying midfielder Moylan as a striker during the past two matches, Cardiff are able to call upon the services of Yousef Salech once again following his shoulder injury.

The Bluebirds are down to the bare bones at the other end of the pitch, though, with Gabriel Osho suspended due to a red card collected at Middlesbrough.

Young defender Dylan Lawlor (hamstring) is sidelined until after the November international break, whilst Calum Chambers continues his recovery from a knee problem.

A reduction of bodies in the Birmingham treatment room over the past month has certainly contributed towards their good form, with Paik Seung-Ho (muscle) the only player possibly unavailable for Saturday.

Despite netting twice off the bench for Blues over the past week, Demarai Gray will be given a substitutes brief once again due to the exceptional performances of fellow winger Millar.

There is more room for debate on the opposite flank, though, where the workman-like Carlos Vicente could replace technician Patrick Roberts for a tricky away fixture.

#2816. « Ils nous ont surclassés » : Aurélien Tchouaméni revient sur la défaite face à l'Espagne

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Dans le dernier épisode de son émission The Bridge, Aurélien Tchouaméni est revenu sans fard sur la désillusion de l'équipe de France face à l'Espagne lors du Mondial 2026. Le milieu tricolore a livré une mise au point sincère sur sa titularisation contestée.

Le mardi 14 juillet 2026 restera une date douloureuse pour le football tricolore. Au Dallas Stadium, devant 70 176 spectateurs, l'équipe de France s'est inclinée face à l'Espagne (2-0) en demi-finale de la Coupe du monde. Les Bleus ont cédé sur un penalty transformé par Mikel Oyarzabal à la 22e minute, avant que Pedro Porro ne scelle le score à la 58e minute. Cette élimination a d'ailleurs constitué le tout dernier match officiel de Didier Deschamps sur le banc tricolore avant son départ. Une élimination marquante.

Plus de trois mois après cette défaite, Aurélien Tchouaméni a analysé la supériorité de la Roja dans son émission. « Si tu regardes bien, ils nous ont dominés comme à l’Euro 2024. T’as l’impression que t’es là, mais ils te montrent qu’ils ont leur plan de jeu clair… Tu crois être à côté, mais si tu regardes, ils nous ont surclassés », a confié l'international français dans The Bridge. Le joueur n'a pas caché l'immense déception vécue dans le vestiaire. « Ce qui était dur, c’est qu’on est tombé de haut, car on a tellement performé jusqu’aux demies, que quand on perd, on s’est dit que notre rêve était terminé », a-t-il ajouté.

Au-delà de la supériorité espagnole, le choix d'aligner le milieu madrilène d'entrée avait suscité de vives critiques. En effet, Aurélien Tchouaméni n'avait plus disputé la moindre minute depuis le seizième de finale face à la Suède en raison d'une blessure. Propulsé titulaire par Didier Deschamps pour cette demi-finale irrespirable, l'ancien Bordelais manquait cruellement de rythme face au milieu ibérique. Un déficit physique évident à ce niveau.

Toutefois, le joueur a expliqué avoir été guidé par l'envie absolue de disputer ce rendez-vous planétaire. « En tant que sportif, les gens doivent le comprendre, il y a de l’adrénaline… Tout le monde rêve de jouer une demi-finale de coupe du monde… Mais la vérité du terrain, c’est la vérité du terrain », a expliqué le milieu du Real Madrid Aurélien Tchouaméni dans The Bridge.

Cible des observateurs après le revers de Dallas, Aurélien Tchouaméni a refusé d'endosser seul la responsabilité de l'élimination. « Je le savais avant le match, tu gagnes tout se passe bien, très bien, « t’as bien fait de revenir ». Tu perds, “ouais pourquoi t’es revenu, on perd à cause de toi”… Un jour ça passe, le jour d’après ça passe pas et tu vas être le bouc émissaire… Non c’est pas comme ça », a affirmé le joueur. Une mise au point très nette.

Désormais, les Bleus débutent un nouveau cycle avec Zinédine Zidane. Le nouveau sélectionneur des Bleus communiquera sa prochaine liste le vendredi 6 novembre 2026 à 18h au siège de la FFF. Les Tricolores affronteront ensuite l'Italie le 12 novembre 2026 à Milan, avant de recevoir la Turquie le 15 novembre 2026 au stade Atlantique de Bordeaux.

#2815. FEATURE | Bayer Leverkusen’s rise from a season of chaos

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Bayer Leverkusen did the unthinkable in 2023/24. They not only stopped Bayern Munich from winning the Bundesliga but also went the season unbeaten in the league to secure their maiden Meisterschale. On top of that, Xabi Alonso’s side added the DFB Pokal title to their trophy cabinet.

But things began to change, especially after the Spaniard’s departure in 2025. With key players like Florian Wirtz, Jeremie Frimpong, and Granit Xhaka leaving, the team slowly fell apart.

In a recent interview with The Athletic, Erik ten Hag, who arrived at the club in the summer of 2025 and was only in charge for three games, revealed that players wanted to leave. “It’s a very strange story,” he says. “They ended an era with Alonso, so all the players wanted to go. We fought for some, and they were not committed anymore. So it was waiting until September 1. The good players signed in the last couple of days. I never worked with them. Three games, and then we parted ways.”

Leverkusen tried to replace the title-winning squad, but they weren’t able to achieve their goals in the 2025/26 season. The team finished sixth in the league under Kasper Hjulmand, who replaced Ten Hag. Failing to qualify for the UEFA Champions League cost the Dane his job.

Carles Martinez was then appointed as head coach in June. Leverkusen sporting director Simon Rolfes stated: “Carles Martinez has successfully developed numerous young players at Toulouse and moulded an international team into a strong unit.”

Rolfes added: “In addition to this valuable experience, he brings a high level of expertise from his time at the Barcelona youth academy to Leverkusen. Carles is a coach with clear principles and a modern approach to the game. We are convinced he can provide the right impetus for our sporting future.”

The start of this season wasn’t ideal for the new coach as his team fell to a 3-2 opening day loss to newly promoted Elversberg. The alarm bells started to ring very quickly; however, in time the team managed to adapt to the coach’s demands. That loss is the only one they have suffered this season. Leverkusen have since managed to remain unbeaten in the Bundesliga and have maintained a 100 percent record in the Europa League – winning seven, and drawing one.

In this run of wins, two players have stood out for Die Werkself. Patrik Schick has shrugged off his injury woes of the past to score six goals and provide two assists in six Bundesliga games this season. He also has two further goals in three Europa League games.

Algerian talent Ibrahim Maza is also turning heads due to his brilliant display and output. The 20-year-old has two goals and two assists to his name in the league and has generally impressed with his skills. On comparisons to Florian Wirtz, Maza told the Bundesliga official website: “Flo is an incredible player, a real Bayer Leverkusen legend. I am still trying to follow my own path and be my own player. I think we are a little different in terms of our playing styles, but of course, it is nice to be able to follow in the footsteps of such a great player. I hope that one day I can play at his level and achieve that.”

As Leverkusen eye a return to Europe’s elite competition, they seem to be on the right path. The team might not be able to replicate the record-breaking season from 2023/24, but recent results indicate that Leverkusen are on the right path after a season of chaos and uncertainty.

#2811. Lewandowski hails Michael Olise as overlooked Ballon d'Or contender alongside Kane and Yamal

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Naim Beneddra | 22 Oct 2026 17:08

Robert Lewandowski has singled out an overlooked Ballon d'Or contender who he believes deserves greater recognition alongside Harry Kane and Lamine Yamal, praising the winger's exceptional decision-making and ability to change matches.

The former Barcelona and Bayern Munich striker has shared his thoughts on the 2026 Ballon d'Or race ahead of Monday's ceremony in London, suggesting that the competition is considerably more open than during the era dominated by Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo.

Lewandowski, who now plays for Chicago Fire, believes several players have legitimate claims to football's most prestigious individual prize, although he has expressed particular admiration for Bayern Munich winger Michael Olise.

The 24-year-old French international has enjoyed an outstanding year under Vincent Kompany, helping Bayern win the Bundesliga and DFB-Pokal before establishing himself as one of the standout performers at the 2026 World Cup.

Olise, who previously represented Crystal Palace in the Premier League, recorded seven assists for France during the tournament, breaking a record previously held by Brazilian legend Pele.

However, despite his extraordinary creativity, the winger has attracted less attention in the Ballon d'Or debate than some of his more prominent rivals.

Speaking in an interview with The Athletic, Lewandowski explained why he considers Olise one of the most influential attacking players in world football, while also acknowledging the achievements of Kane and Yamal.

Lewandowski knows what is required to succeed at Bayern, having scored 344 Bundesliga goals during his eight seasons with the German champions.

The Polish international has been particularly impressed by Olise's impact since the Frenchman joined Bayern from Crystal Palace in 2024.

"Olise, for me, is like: 'Wow.' He's the game-changer player," Lewandowski told The Athletic.

The 38-year-old went on to compare Olise's influence with that of Yamal, highlighting the importance of wide players who can decide matches through their individual quality.

He also included Kane in that discussion, recognising the England captain's remarkable goalscoring contribution to Bayern.

However, it is Olise's intelligence and decision-making that appear to have left the strongest impression on Lewandowski.

"His decisions are perfect," he explained, praising the Frenchman's ability to recognise when to shoot, when to pass and which type of delivery a particular situation requires.

The former Bayern forward also made clear how much he enjoys watching Olise, describing him as a player with an exceptionally bright future.

Those compliments are supported by the winger's performances during the 2025-26 campaign.

Olise scored 22 goals and registered 26 assists in 52 appearances across all competitions, according to Opta's statistical records.

He finished as the Bundesliga's leading assist provider with 19, while adding six more in the Champions League as Bayern reached the semi-finals.

The Frenchman also collected the Bundesliga Player of the Season award after playing a leading role in his club's domestic success.

His achievements were particularly significant given the attacking competition at Bayern, where Kane continued to deliver extraordinary goalscoring numbers.

Under Kompany, Olise has developed into a creative focal point, combining his dribbling ability with an increasing capacity to dictate the tempo and direction of attacking moves.

Lewandowski's assessment therefore extends beyond the winger's impressive statistics, focusing on the qualities that allow him to influence games even when he does not score.

Despite his admiration for Olise, Lewandowski has not suggested that the Frenchman should necessarily finish ahead of every other contender.

The former Barcelona striker has also expressed his support for Yamal, who helped Spain win the World Cup after enjoying another outstanding campaign with the Catalan giants.

Kane, meanwhile, has strengthened his candidacy through his exceptional performances for Bayern, where he has established himself as one of the most prolific strikers in European football.

Lewandowski believes Olise's relative lack of recognition may partly reflect his failure to score at the World Cup.

Although France finished fourth, the winger emerged as the tournament's outstanding creative force.

His seven assists surpassed Pele's previous benchmark of six, established during Brazil's victorious 1970 campaign.

Olise produced that record-breaking seventh assist during France's third-place play-off against England, having already created numerous opportunities for teammates throughout the tournament.

Lewandowski suggested that adding one or two goals to those performances could have significantly strengthened the winger's position in the Ballon d'Or discussion.

The comparison is particularly interesting given the emphasis frequently placed on goalscoring and decisive moments when individual awards are determined.

Olise did not find the net at the World Cup, but his creative contribution was central to France's attacking performances, demonstrating an influence that cannot be measured by goals alone.

His rise has also been remarkable from an English football perspective.

After emerging through Reading's academy, Olise established himself at Crystal Palace, scoring 10 Premier League goals in his final season at Selhurst Park before completing his move to Bayern.

He has since developed into one of Europe's most productive attacking players, earning recognition for both his individual performances and his ability to create opportunities for others.

Olise's exceptional campaign has continued into the 2026-27 season, further strengthening the argument that he belongs among the game's elite attacking talents.

Whether he receives significant support in Monday's Ballon d'Or vote remains to be seen, with Kane, Yamal and Kylian Mbappe among the other leading contenders.

For Lewandowski, however, Olise's performances have already demonstrated the qualities of a player capable of competing for football's biggest individual honours in the years ahead.

Présent à Austin pour disputer le Grand Prix des États-Unis, Max Verstappen a vivement critiqué l'état du Circuit des Amériques. Le quadruple champion du monde néerlandais a jugé les bosses inacceptables et s'inquiète pour sa colonne vertébrale.

Alors que la course du Grand Prix des États-Unis est programmée ce dimanche 25 octobre à 21 heures (heure française), Max Verstappen arrive en grande forme au Texas, avec une série de cinq podiums consécutifs, incluant une victoire lors de la dernière course à Singapour. Toutefois, l'état de la piste texane lui pose problème dès son arrivée dans le paddock.

Lors d'un point presse tenu le jeudi 22 octobre 2026, avant le Grand Prix des États-Unis disputé sur le Circuit des Amériques (COTA) à Austin, Max Verstappen n'a pas caché sa colère. Le tracé texan repose en effet sur un sol argileux qui a tendance à bouger selon les conditions météorologiques, ce qui accentue les déformations au fil du temps. Une situation dénoncée par le quadruple champion du monde, couronné consécutivement en 2021, 2022, 2023 et 2024.

Ces dernières années, ils ont essayé de les réduire par-ci par-là mais c'est juste mettre un pansement sur un problème bien plus gros, ce qu'il y a en dessous de la piste. Toutefois, ces aménagements partiels restent vains aux yeux du pilote de Milton Keynes, qui estime ces interventions insuffisantes pour stabiliser le sous-sol.

Dans le paddock, le constat suscite d'ailleurs des réactions diverses.
