#2840. Bayern release update on Kompany after collapse

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Bayern Munich have provided an update on Vincent Kompany after the Belgian coach suddenly collapsed towards the end of a training session on Friday.

“Vincent Kompany had to receive medical attention on Friday after collapsing during a training session involving FC Bayern’s reserve team. The first-team manager had been watching the second team’s training session,” the club stated.

“Kompany is undergoing thorough examinations in hospital and will remain under medical supervision. Until further notice, his coaching staff will take charge of training sessions and matches.”

Bayern board member for sport Max Eberl commented:

“Vincent Kompany’s health is our highest priority. At FC Bayern, we will do everything we can to ensure that he recovers quickly. Vinnie is receiving all the necessary medical care and support, as well as all the time he needs. His coaching staff, whom we trust completely, will take over his duties. All of us at FC Bayern wish Vinnie a speedy recovery.”

Kompany has recently been battling a viral infection and hip problems. As a result, he had to sit down during Bayern’s 6-1 win over RB Leipzig last week.

He also missed the final training session and pre-match press conference ahead of Wednesday’s 2-2 draw against Arsenal, but returned to the dugout to lead his team during the match.

FC Bayern face SC Freiburg away from home in the Bundesliga on Sunday.

#2839. « Faire du vélo, c'est plus facile que de travailler à l'usine » : la mise au point sans filtre de Bernard Hinault

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Toujours aussi direct dans ses prises de parole, Bernard Hinault s’est confié sans détour à l'approche de sa venue en Normandie. Le quintuple vainqueur du Tour de France a balayé l'idée d'une souffrance insurmontable dans le peloton, comparant avec franchise le métier de coureur cycliste au travail à l'usine.

Bernard Hinault demeure une légende du cyclisme tricolore, quintuple vainqueur du Tour de France (1978, 1979, 1981, 1982, 1985) et toujours le dernier coureur français à avoir remporté l'épreuve. Alors qu'il fêtera ses 72 ans le 14 novembre 2026, l'ancien champion vit désormais retiré près de Dinan, dans les Côtes-d'Armor, à l'écart du monde du cyclisme depuis dix ans. Interrogé par L'Éveil Normand sur la souffrance physique dans le cyclisme de haut niveau, le champion s'est montré très clair.

Bernard Hinault a insisté sur le plaisir de ce métier, soulignant la chance d'exercer sa passion, la liberté de voyager et le fait d'être particulièrement bien payé. Une mise au point sans détour.

Bernard Hinault pourra d'ailleurs bientôt échanger avec le public. Bernard Hinault sera en effet présent le samedi 24 octobre 2026 à Thiberville, dans l'Eure, en tant qu'invité d'honneur de la 10e édition de la Ronde thibervillaise. L'ancien champion italien Gianni Bugno, ancien vainqueur du Tour d'Italie et double champion du monde sur route, est également invité d'honneur de ce rassemblement.

C'est vrai que ce sont deux invités de marque. Ils m'ont dit oui sans détour. La Ronde thibervillaise est réputée, je pense, pour être une manifestation sans chichi et ça leur va bien, a confié Bruno Thouroude, l'organisateur de la Ronde thibervillaise, ancien cycliste amateur et commerçant de vélos, avec qui Bernard Hinault est resté en contact après une première visite.

Au-delà de son propre parcours, Bernard Hinault a également partagé son regard sur les jeunes coureurs du peloton contemporain. Interrogé sur des espoirs comme le Français Paul Seixas (20 ans) et le Belge Remco Evenepoel (26 ans), il a estimé qu'ils font partie d'une génération brillante capable d'aller loin à condition de surmonter la fatigue mentale, avec pour unique conseil de se faire plaisir.

Le public pourra retrouver les champions dès ce samedi 24 octobre 2026 à Thiberville. Le programme comprend des dédicaces dès 9h30, la présentation des écoles de cyclisme de l'Eure à 14h, une randonnée cycliste à allure libre vers 14h30 et une soirée cabaret avec dîner pour 250 à 300 personnes à 19h30 à la salle des fêtes.

Pompey recorded back-to-back victories over Sheffield United and Norwich City, before they rescued a 1-1 draw in their first meeting with Wolverhampton Wanderers in 16 years.

They will now return to the familiar surroundings of Fratton Park, where they have won two and drawn one of their last three matches, scoring at least two goals in each of those games.

Portsmouth will hope that home advantage can help them claim a third consecutive win against Millwall after recording a pair of 3-1 victories in both meetings in the 2025-26 Championship season.

Millwall are sitting one place above Portsmouth on goals scored, but they have plenty of work to do if they are to get anywhere near matching last season's third-placed finish.

The Lions have won just two of their last nine league matches after starting the season with back-to-back victories.

The most recent of those victories came via a narrow 1-0 scoreline in their away clash against Preston North End earlier this month.

Millwall then came out of the wrong side of a 1-0 result in their home meeting with Swansea City, before they fought back from two goals down to salvage a 2-2 draw against Lincoln City at The Den, their third stalemate in the last five matches.

After playing consecutive home games, Millwall head on their travels with hopes of recording back-to-back league away wins for the first time since March.

Prior to last season, the Lions had enjoyed a run of six consecutive victories against Portsmouth in all competitions, a winning run that also yielded four clean sheets.

Striker Colby Bishop is unlikely to be ready after being left out of Tuesday's matchday squad with a knee issue.

Marlon Pack, Ryan Kavuma-McQueen, Marezi and Zak Swanson are all pushing for recalls after dropping down to the bench in midweek.

As for the visitors, goalkeeper Max Crocombe will start in place of Filip Marschall, who is set for a spell on the sidelines after sustaining an ankle injury in the draw with Lincoln.

The Lions are also without the services of Lukas Jensen, Massimo Luongo, Casper De Norre, Mathis Servais, Mihailo Ivanovic and Josh Coburn.

#2833. Real Madrid : les idées de José Mourinho pour battre le FC Barcelone dimanche

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

À l'approche du déplacement au Camp Nou, José Mourinho prépare trois plans tactiques en 4-2-3-1 pour renverser le FC Barcelone. Devancé en Liga, l'entraîneur du Real Madrid prépare des ajustements pour relancer son équipe dans la course au titre.

Ce premier Clásico officiel opposant José Mourinho à Hansi Flick se dispute dans le cadre de la 10e journée de LaLiga, le dimanche 25 octobre 2026 à 21h00 au Spotify Camp Nou. Vainqueur face au RB Leipzig en Ligue des champions le mercredi 21 octobre 2026 (4-1), le Real Madrid aborde ce rendez-vous capital avec une réelle obligation de résultat.

La situation comptable ne laisse en effet que peu de marge aux Merengue avant ce déplacement en Catalogne. Troisième du championnat avec 21 points au compteur, le Real Madrid compte six longueurs de retard sur son rival barcelonais. Invaincu en tête de LaLiga, le FC Barcelone totalise 27 points en neuf victoires, avec une différence de buts de +30. Pour rester dans la course au titre, les Madrilènes doivent impérativement s'imposer sur la pelouse de leur concurrent direct.

Interrogé après la victoire contre Leipzig sur la titularisation de Federico Valverde comme latéral droit, le technicien portugais a refusé d'aborder le match de dimanche. « Non… je ne parle pas de dimanche. Si tu me poses la même question samedi, je te répondrai. Je suis très honnête », a ainsi affirmé José Mourinho en conférence de presse.

Afin de faire tomber le Barça, José Mourinho étudie trois plans tactiques en 4-2-3-1 articulés autour du positionnement de Federico Valverde. Dans le plan A, l'Uruguayen conserverait le poste de latéral droit occupé lors des deux derniers matches. Cette option permettrait de verrouiller le couloir face à Anthony Gordon, devançant Trent Alexander-Arnold et Denzel Dumfries. Dans ce schéma, Arda Güler serait associé à Aurélien Tchouaméni, avec Endrick ou Bernardo Silva sur l'aile droite.

Toutefois, deux autres options restent à l'étude. Dans le plan B, Federico Valverde retrouverait son poste au milieu de terrain aux côtés de Tchouaméni. Un latéral droit de métier, Dumfries ou Alexander-Arnold, entrerait alors et Güler glisserait sur l'aile droite. Enfin, dans le plan C, Valverde jouerait ailier droit pour renforcer le couloir droit avec un arrière de métier, Güler reprenant une position axiale.

Cette réflexion tactique s'accompagne d'une attaque remaniée. Vinícius Júnior est suspendu pour ce Clásico afin de purger le deuxième match de sa sanction disciplinaire. L'attaquant Yan Diomandé est ainsi attendu comme titulaire sur le flanc gauche madrilène pour compenser cette absence. À en croire la presse espagnole, sept joueurs sont indiscutables : Thibaut Courtois, Ibrahima Konaté, Marc Cucurella, Aurélien Tchouaméni, Jude Bellingham, Yan Diomandé et Kylian Mbappé.

De retour dans l'axe après avoir été mis au repos contre Leipzig, Ibrahima Konaté attend encore de connaître son binôme, le coach hésitant entre Antonio Rüdiger et Dean Huijsen. Le dernier poste en suspens concerne l'option offensive entre Güler, Bernardo Silva et Endrick. D'ailleurs, selon Marca, Eduardo Camavinga pourrait intégrer l'entrejeu pour exploiter ses transitions et son impact physique face au bloc catalan.

#2832. “We won't change our transfer policy”: Uli Hoeneß confirms Bayern plan long-term with Viessmann investment

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Earlier this week, FC Bayern confirmed Viessmann Generations Group will join Adidas, Audi and Allianz as major external shareholders of the club. In an exclusive interview with “Welt am Sonntag”, FC Bayern's honorary president Uli Hoeneß discussed the matter with his usual frankness.

“This money isn't intended for the immediate purpose of squad building. It's a long-term investment and won't automatically lead us to titles,” Hoeneß started his explanation (via @iMiaSanMia). “We won't be changing our transfer policy either. And that's not necessary. Clubs that spend money like crazy always end up getting burned.

“I'm thinking of Liverpool, who spent around €500 million and only finished fifth in the Premier League. Even Paris Saint-Germain only won the Champions League after they started making sensible investments. And that was without Neymar and Kylian Mbappé. Good scouting and developing players are the keys to success.”

Hoeneß also talked about next season's Champions League final, which will be played at Bayern's ground, Allianz Arena. The 74-year-old was the club's president when they lost the previous “Finale dahoam” against Chelsea.

“Reaching the final is always the goal. And reaching the final in Munich is a particularly special dream. But you can't plan something like that." said the controversial figure in a changed tone from his demanding quote back in 2012.

“You shouldn’t reduce everything to winning a trophy. So much can happen - suspensions, injuries. When I hear people say, ‘The treble is the only objective,’ it frustrates me. We have a different philosophy from clubs where money is no object, where hedge funds or entire countries finance the club.

“We now have more than 450,000 members. When I joined FC Bayern in 1979, we had 8,000. A third of our members have never been able to watch one of our matches in our stadium because it is always sold out, and yet our membership continues to grow relentlessly. That is an enormous success."

Hamburg head coach Merlin Polzin has received a welcome personnel boost ahead of Saturday’s Bundesliga trip to SC Paderborn. Patson Daka, Albert Sambi Lokonga, and Bakery Jatta are all available again after recovering from their respective injury problems. The returning trio provide the 35-year-old with additional options as Hamburger SV attempt to secure a fourth consecutive Bundesliga victory.

Speaking at Thursday’s pre-match press conference, Polzin confirmed that the three players had participated extensively in training during the week, with some completing full sessions. Captain Nicolai Remberg and the suspended Mario Vuskovic remain unavailable, but the coach was otherwise pleased with his squad’s improving fitness situation.

The welcome developments nevertheless present Polzin with something of a selection dilemma. Following HSV’s dramatic 1-0 victory over VfB Stuttgart last weekend, the Hanseaten have now won three consecutive Bundesliga matches for the first time in 16 years.

“For us, this is an outstanding situation to be in,” Polzin said on Thursday. “We have players who are performing, delivering results, and helping the team succeed. Competition for places does something to a team.”

The coach emphasized that his returning players must earn their opportunities through their performances in training. He also praised the contributions of his substitutes, who have repeatedly influenced matches during HSV’s recent winning streak.

“Everyone is eager and making it very difficult for me to decide who will start,” Polzin said.

HSV’s improving fortunes represent a remarkable reversal from the opening weeks of the campaign. Polzin’s team lost their first three Bundesliga fixtures without scoring a goal, conceding 12 times in defeats against Borussia Dortmund, Mainz 05, and RB Leipzig. Despite the disastrous start, Hamburg’s leadership reaffirmed its faith in Polzin by extending the contracts of the entire coaching staff.

The decision came immediately before the fourth matchday, when HSV finally secured their first victory of the season against 1. FC Köln. A dramatic 3-2 comeback victory away at Hoffenheim followed, before Swiss international Miro Muheim struck another spectacular late winner against Stuttgart.

The improvement has nevertheless come at a significant personnel cost. Remberg sustained a serious knee injury during the Hoffenheim encounter, subsequently underwent surgery for a torn ACL, and faces a lengthy spell on the sidelines. His absence, combined with Sambi Lokonga’s muscular problems, forced Polzin to reconsider his midfield arrangements.

The HSV coach has repeatedly emphasized the importance of tactical flexibility, having already altered his team’s approach several times since assuming responsibility in November 2024. Polzin initially sought to introduce a more progressive possession-based game during the summer, but HSV’s dreadful start prompted a renewed emphasis on defensive stability and compactness.

The effects have been striking. Hamburg averaged only slightly more than 37 percent possession across their recent three-match winning streak, including just 34 percent against Stuttgart. The personnel adjustments have been equally important.

With Remberg and Sambi Lokonga unavailable, Polzin deployed Martin Adeline, Bilal Nadir, and Muheim in an unfamiliar central midfield configuration against Stuttgart. Muheim’s emergence has been particularly significant.

The 28-year-old began the campaign struggling to reclaim his customary position on the left flank following injury problems and increased competition from David Møller Wolfe and Louis Lemke. The Swiss international nevertheless embraced his new central role and has now scored decisive goals in consecutive Bundesliga victories.

His remarkable turnaround has become emblematic of HSV’s collective recovery.

Polzin has also been particularly encouraged by the impact of his substitutes. Against Stuttgart, Albert Grønbæk and Zakaria El Ouahdi entered the match in the 78th minute and combined to create Muheim’s late winner. The coach believes opponents are beginning to recognize the threat HSV pose during the closing stages of matches.

“When the opposing team knows that HSV steps things up towards the end of a match, that’s of incredible value,” Polzin explained.

The returns of Daka, Sambi Lokonga, and Jatta could provide another boost, although Polzin has given no indication that all three will immediately return to the starting XI. Sambi Lokonga offers a potential solution to HSV’s midfield absences, while Daka and Jatta increase the coach’s attacking options.

Saturday’s opponents Paderborn have collected just five points from their opening six Bundesliga fixtures.

#2826. Como vs Sassuolo - prediction, team news, lineups

By Jonathan O'Shea | 22 Oct 2026 16:44

After an historic midweek victory in Europe, Como must try to post back-to-back Serie A wins when Sassuolo visit Stadio Sinigaglia on Saturday.

Having brushed Fiorentina aside last week, the Lariani easily beat Manchester United in the Champions League, plunging the English giants into another crisis.

Never in any trouble after Nico Paz struck within just two minutes, Como ultimately cruised past Man United on Wednesday, as they made it two 4-1 wins from as many Champions League home games.

When United were last crowned European champions in 2008, the Lariani were languishing down in Serie D, but so much has changed since then for both clubs.

Sending the Sinigaglia wild, an inspired Paz scored twice and set up Tasos Douvikas for another, before Jacobo Ramon headed home number four - all of which leaves Como well placed to reach the knockout phase.

The continental debutants have also posted 14 points in Serie A, equalling a club-record start set in 1950 with last week's 3-1 win over Fiorentina.

Inevitably, Paz was on target again at Stadio Franchi, as the Lariani responded to rare back-to-back defeats with a convincing performance.

Having already penned several new pages in Como's long history, Cesc Fabregas and his squad are still unbeaten on home turf this term, and they may even be eyeing the title race.

While Como dumped Sassuolo out of the Coppa Italia last season, then won the clubs' first-ever Serie A meeting, the Neroverdi responded with a 2-1 home victory in April.

That might give them hope ahead of this trip to Lombardy, even if recent results definitely do not.

While Como have been busy breaking new ground, Alberto Aquilani's side have taken just one point from their last three matches, following a 4-2 defeat to newly promoted Frosinone.

With six mistakes so far, Sassuolo have made more errors leading to a goal than any other team, and goalkeeper Arijanet Muric set the tone with his third of the season last weekend.

The Neroverdi were always on the back foot after conceding inside the first 10 minutes, and that loss dropped them to 13th place in the Serie A standings.

So, aside from a promising cup run - they will meet Juventus for a last-16 tie in early December - Aquilani's reign has proved a little underwhelming.

Como are currently missing Trevoh Chalobah, Jesus Rodriguez and Assane Diao through injury, while Mattia Liberali was unable to play on Wednesday due to a shin problem.

The latter should be back in contention this weekend, when Moise Kean could also return: if sufficiently fit, the Italy striker might deputise for Douvikas, who has regularly started amid a busy spell of fixtures.   Though he struck against Man United, Douvikas - who also scored against Sassuolo in league and cup last term - has netted all of his last six league goals away from home.

While Paz reached 20 Serie A goals when he scored against Fiorentina, Sassuolo's star man is somewhat further ahead.

Including his recent strike against Frosinone, Domenico Berardi has notched 132 top-flight goals since 2013 - only Ciro Immobile (196) and Lautaro Martinez (136) have managed more during that period.

With plenty of competition in attack, Sebastiano Esposito and Kieron Bowie will continue to fight for one place up front.

Once again, Ismael Kone, Jay Idzes, Daniel Boloca and Belgian defender Fedde Leysen are all unavailable.

#2825. « Pas digne d'un match de Coupe d'Europe » : Bruno Genesio se lâche après la nouvelle défaite de l'OM

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Bruno Genesio n'a pas caché son immense déception après le revers concédé par l'Olympique de Marseille sur le terrain du Sturm Graz. L'entraîneur marseillais a poussé un coup de gueule remarqué contre l'attitude défaillante affichée par ses joueurs durant les quarante-cinq premières minutes en Autriche.

Ce jeudi 22 octobre 2026, à l'occasion de la 3e journée de la phase de ligue de Ligue Europa, Marseille s'est incliné contre Sturm Graz (1-0). Cueillis à froid à la Merkur-Arena, les Olympiens ont cédé dès la 13e minute de jeu sur une réalisation d'Oumar Diakité, consécutive à une perte de balle plein axe de Nouhoum Kamissoko.

Présent devant les médias à l'issue de la rencontre, le technicien marseillais a exprimé une vive colère quant à la prestation de ses troupes. Visiblement excédé par le visage offert en première période, Bruno Genesio a dénoncé l'absence de réaction de son groupe dans l'engagement physique. Un constat cinglant. « On a fait une première période insipide, pas digne d'un match de Coupe d'Europe. En Coupe d'Europe, le premier ingrédient qu'on doit mettre c'est l'engagement, l'agressivité, gagner les duels. Et en première mi-temps, on n'a pas existé dans ce domaine-là. En deuxième mi-temps, on a pris un peu plus d'initiative, on a eu une domination territoriale très importante mais sans se créer d'occasions nettes par rapport à cette domination », a déclaré Bruno Genesio en conférence de presse après la défaite contre Sturm Graz.

« Depuis le début de saison, c'est peut-être la mi-temps qui me déçoit le plus », a insisté Bruno Genesio en conférence de presse. Pour tenter d'inverser la dynamique, l'entraîneur phocéen a d'ailleurs frappé fort dès la pause en remplaçant simultanément Neal Maupay et Angel Gomes par Himad Abdelli et Igor Paixão.

Si le but autrichien est venu d'une bévue individuelle du jeune défenseur central Nouhoum Kamissoko (21 ans), Bruno Genesio a tenu à protéger son joueur pour reporter la faute sur l'état d'esprit global. « Je suis déçu de la performance de mon équipe, je m'attendais à mieux après ce qu'on avait montré contre Angers et même contre l'Olympiacos. Je suis surtout déçu par la première mi-temps, on peut rater des choses. Par exemple, je ne vais pas en vouloir à Kami (Kamissoko) d'avoir perdu un ballon sur le but, ça fait partie du football, ça fait partie de l'apprentissage, derrière il s'est remis dedans. Mais je suis déçu de l'attitude qu'on a montrée en première mi-temps. On a été dominés dans tous les duels, on s'est fait tamponner dans tous les points chauds, on n'a pas répondu dans ce domaine-là », a détaillé Bruno Genesio en conférence de presse d'après-match.

En effet, les statistiques illustrent la stérilité marseillaise malgré une nette possession. Les Phocéens ont ainsi totalisé 72 % de possession et 14 tirs au cours de la partie, mais n'ont cadré qu'une seule tentative, tandis que Keyliane Abdallah a trouvé le poteau à la 39e minute de jeu.

Au-delà de cette déconvenue en terre autrichienne, la situation devient particulièrement alarmante sur la scène européenne. L'Olympique de Marseille enregistre ainsi son troisième revers en trois rencontres de Ligue Europa cette saison, après les défaites subies face au Besiktas (4-1) et à l'Olympiakos (0-1). Avec un zéro pointé et une inquiétante 34e place au classement, le bilan contraste nettement avec le championnat, où le club marseillais restait sur deux victoires probantes contre Troyes (2-0) et Angers (5-2).

Les hommes de Bruno Genesio devront réagir dès ce week-end. L'OM recevra Le Havre AC au stade Vélodrome le dimanche 25 octobre 2026 pour la 9e journée de Ligue 1, avant d'aborder un déplacement périlleux sur le terrain du Bayer Leverkusen le jeudi 5 novembre 2026 pour la 4e journée de Ligue Europa.

#2824. Lyon sombre à Hoffenheim, mais Paulo Fonseca assume ses choix

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

L'entraîneur de l'OL Paulo Fonseca a pleinement assumé son large turnover après la défaite concédée contre le TSG Hoffenheim (3-0) ce jeudi soir en Ligue Europa. Un choix stratégique délibéré pour préserver ses cadres en vue du choc au sommet face au Paris Saint-Germain en Ligue 1.

Ce jeudi 22 octobre 2026, à l'occasion de la 3e journée de Ligue Europa à la PreZero Arena de Sinsheim, Paulo Fonseca a pris le pari de bouleverser son onze de départ. Plusieurs cadres lyonnais ont ainsi été laissés sur le banc des remplaçants au coup d'envoi. Paulo Fonseca a surpris en titularisant d'entrée deux jeunes de l'académie, Rémi Himbert et Steeve Kango. Une prise de risque maximale. Toutefois, le jeune défenseur Steeve Kango a connu un coup d'arrêt précoce en sortant sur blessure dès la première période après une mauvaise chute sur le dos.

Au micro des médias après la défaite contre Hoffenheim, le coach de l'Olympique Lyonnais a immédiatement justifié cette gestion d'effectif pour aborder les rendez-vous nationaux avec des forces intactes : « On a géré les temps de jeu de l'équipe car nous avons un match très important pour nous et très difficile contre le PSG. Donc je dois gérer les temps de jeu ».

Sur le terrain, cette large rotation a débouché sur un lourd revers pour les Gones. Face à une formation allemande réaliste, les Lyonnais ont cédé avant la pause face à Andrej Kramarić (45e+4), avant d'encaisser deux nouveaux buts signés Adam Daghim (49e) et Adam Hložek (83e). Poison pour l'arrière-garde lyonnaise, Andrej Kramarić a été impliqué sur les trois buts de la rencontre avec une réalisation et deux passes décisives. Hoffenheim a d'ailleurs dominé les débats avec 21 tirs dont 8 cadrés (2,08 xG), contre seulement 8 tirs dont 2 cadrés (0,66 xG) pour l'OL, malgré une possession de balle de 57% en faveur des Lyonnais.

Ce résultat marque le premier revers européen de la saison pour le club rhodanien, qui restait sur deux victoires consécutives face au RSC Anderlecht (2-1) et Crystal Palace (3-2). Face aux journalistes après la rencontre, Paulo Fonseca a publiquement déploré l'attitude et le manque d'intensité de ses joueurs : « La façon dont on devait jouer ici était claire, j'avais beaucoup parlé avec les joueurs. Mais le message n'est peut-être pas passé, je ne sais pas… ».

Au-delà de cette déconvenue en Allemagne, l'Olympique Lyonnais a désormais le regard tourné vers le championnat de France. L'OL occupe la place de leader de Ligue 1 avant de défier son dauphin parisien pour le compte de la 8e journée, le PSG n'étant devancé qu'à la différence de buts. Un choc capital pour défendre la première place de l'élite.

Cette confrontation au sommet entre le PSG et l'OL, initialement prévue le dimanche 25 octobre 2026 à 20h45 au Parc des Princes, demeure toutefois entourée d'incertitude. La rencontre pourrait être reportée de 24 heures en raison de la mobilisation des forces de l'ordre à travers le pays.

#2822. Paderborn celebrate revival of 28-year-old striker Steffen Tigges: “Tiggi 2.0”

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

SC Paderborn head coach Ralf Kettemann has confirmed that Steffen Tigges is expected to retain his place in the starting XI for Saturday’s Bundesliga encounter against Hamburg. The 28-year-old striker has impressed his coach with a remarkable improvement in form, earning another opportunity after contributing a goal and an assist in last weekend’s 2-2 draw against Werder Bremen.

Speaking at Thursday’s pre-match press conference, Kettemann made a rare exception to his usual policy of avoiding definitive statements about his starting lineup. The Paderborn trainer hopes that Tigges can maintain the momentum generated by his recent performances, highlighting the striker’s increasing confidence and consistent improvement on the training pitch.

“He’s simply a different Tiggi, a better Tiggi with more self-confidence,” Kettemann explained. “That simply deserved to be rewarded.”

The public endorsement represents another significant step in Tigges’ efforts to reestablish himself as a Bundesliga striker following several difficult seasons at Cologne.

Kettemann’s decision to select Tigges ahead of Marvin Pieringer last weekend proved particularly effective. The former Borussia Dortmund striker contributed to both Paderborn goals as the newly promoted side established a 2-0 first-half lead at the Weserstadion. Tigges helped create Stefano Marino’s 18th-minute opener before doubling the visitors’ advantage himself following a defensive mistake.

Werder Bremen head coach Daniel Thioune was particularly frustrated by the circumstances surrounding Tigges’ goal.

“Nobody on my team wanted the ball,” Thioune remarked during his post-match press conference. “Steffen Tigges wanted the ball and he scored the goal.”

Bremen eventually recovered through two second-half goals from Niclas Füllkrug, leaving Paderborn to settle for a point despite their impressive opening period. Tigges’ individual contribution nevertheless represented further evidence of a resurgence that Kettemann insists has been developing for several months.

Speaking before the Bremen encounter, the coach explained that he and Tigges had worked together on refining the striker’s game.

“Tiggi has developed wonderfully since the summer break,” Kettemann told DAZN. “We’ve discussed a few things to bring out Tiggi 2.0. There’s more in him.”

Following the match, Kettemann reiterated that the striker had been demonstrating excellent form throughout the Bundesliga campaign.

“He believes that he can still develop at 28 and do a few things differently to become more dangerous in front of goal,” the Paderborn coach explained.

Tigges’ recent improvement is particularly significant given the circumstances surrounding his departure from Köln.

The Osnabrück native initially broke into Borussia Dortmund’s senior squad under Edin Terzic in December 2020, having established an impressive goalscoring record for the club’s reserve team. He scored three Bundesliga goals during the 2021/22 campaign under Marco Rose before moving to Cologne in the summer of 2022 for a reported €1.5m fee.

The transfer initially appeared promising. Tigges scored seven Bundesliga goals and contributed three assists during his first season with die Geißböcke. His performances included memorable goals against former club Dortmund, a brace in Köln’s emphatic 7-1 victory over Werder Bremen, and an important contribution against Augsburg.

His subsequent development nevertheless proved considerably more difficult. The striker struggled for consistency during Köln’s disastrous 2023/24 relegation campaign and gradually lost his place in the team. He still provided some memorable late interventions, including important goals against Bochum and Union Berlin, but could not prevent the club’s relegation.

A return to the 2. Bundesliga offered little improvement. Tigges failed to score in 18 competitive appearances during the 2024/25 season and found himself surplus to requirements following Köln’s promotion back to the top flight.

New head coach Lukas Kwasniok even experimented with deploying him on the left wing during the summer of 2025, but the trial failed to secure him a meaningful role in the squad. Köln ultimately sold Tigges to Paderborn for a reported €400,000 in August 2025.

The substantially reduced fee reflected how far Tigges’ standing had fallen since his initial move from Dortmund. Tigges nevertheless approached the change positively, explaining that his difficult experiences at Köln had shaped him and that he was looking forward to a fresh challenge.

The first encouraging signs of Tigges’ renewed form emerged during Paderborn’s preparations for the 2026/27 Bundesliga campaign. As noted in Get German Football News’ comprehensive SC Paderborn tactical preview, the striker enjoyed an especially productive preseason, including a hat-trick in a 4-3 friendly victory over his former club Köln.

The performance suggested that Tigges might once again become a valuable Bundesliga attacking option, although his prospects of establishing himself in Kettemann’s starting lineup remained uncertain. Paderborn possessed several alternatives, including the more mobile Stefano Marino, whose tendency to drop into deeper positions offered the coach a different interpretation of the central striking role.

Marvin Pieringer subsequently emerged as another important competitor for attacking minutes. Tigges nevertheless continued to make his case through training performances and substitute appearances. His first Bundesliga goal of the season came during Paderborn’s 3-1 victory over Hoffenheim in September. He subsequently maintained his positive form despite initially remaining outside the starting XI.

The contrast with the striker’s situation at Köln a little over a year ago could scarcely be greater.

A player who had struggled to find competitive minutes in the second division is now earning the public backing of a Bundesliga coach who believes his development remains far from complete. Saturday’s home encounter against Hamburg offers Tigges another opportunity to justify that confidence.

For a striker whose Bundesliga prospects appeared to be fading during his final months in Cologne, Kettemann’s talk of a newer, better version of “Tiggi” represents a particularly welcome change.
