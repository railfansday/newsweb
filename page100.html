📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Published: 20:00 BST, 21 October 2026 | Updated: 15:24 BST, 22 October 2026

As the full-time whistle blew on one of Manchester United’s most embarrassing nights in Europe, Michael Carrick walked over alone to the few away fans still left at the Giuseppe Sinigaglia Stadium and held up his hands in apology.

They had stood drenched for two hours, battered by the wind and rain that whipped off Lake Como. This has to be the most picturesque venues in the Champions League by some distance, but on a night like this it was downright ugly and so were United.

Carrick’s apology was reminiscent of Ole Gunnar Solskjaer’s farewell game at Watford in November 2021, which also ended in a humbling 4-1 defeat.

Solskjaer knew he was a dead man walking that day. If this proves to be Carrick’s last stand, what a lame surrender it was.

How much longer can this go on? How much longer can United sleepwalk through this wretched season as Carrick continues to grasp hopelessly for positives?

Statistically the fifth worst European defeat in United’s history was also Carrick’s fifth in 11 games this wretched season as they were torn apart by Como and the excellent Nico Paz.

Manchester United were completely outplayed by Como as they lost 4-1 to the Italian side

The dreadful result heaps further pressure on under-fire Red Devils boss Michael Carrick

This was an incredible night for Como, but one of the worst in United's European history

This was meant to be the biggest game in Como’s history and they played like it. Cesc Fabregas’ team are a credit to the former Arsenal midfielder and it’s not by chance that they lie fifth in Serie A.

But Manchester United coming to town is no longer what it was. This is a pathetic imitation of the teams that have represented this great club down the years.

The 700 fans who made the journey to Lombardy stood in one of the areas of this stadium exposed to the elements beneath Giuseppe Terragni’s Monumento ai Caduti that sits at the head of the lake. It means monument to the fallen, and how much further can United keep falling from here?

They were insipid. Lifeless. The confidence and self-belief has been sucked out of this team. It’s eight games since they last won, beating Sabah of Azerbaijan in this competition and recording their only clean sheet of the season.
