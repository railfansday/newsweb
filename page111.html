#1457. How Jaedyn Shaw is holding Gotham's attack together during NWSL Shield pursuit

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

Is Jaedyn Shaw the one thing keeping Gotham FC's season together? While this may feel like a strange question to pose, for a team that's en route to winning the NWSL Shield for the most points in the regular season, Gotham's stats have been quite odd. As Gotham prepare to travel to face the Washington Spirit on Saturday, it's worth taking a look at those Shield credentials with three games left in the regular season.

Unfortunately, there's a chance that we could get an answer to this question during the match. Shaw left United States U-23 international duty with a minor injury and is considered day-to-day at this stage. When asked about her availability for this top-of-the-table clash, Gotham FC head coach Juan Carlos Amoros simply said, "Wait and see." Gotham have conceded the fewest goals in the league while also scoring the 12th-most goals of any team. They have the second-best goal difference due to their defensive play, but when looking deeper at the numbers, they aren't clearly the best team in the league; that's likely the San Diego Wave. By the numbers, the team chasing them has the better goal difference and expected goals difference.

Of course, soccer is played on grass and not spreadsheets, but this is important to take note of, with the playoffs around the corner. That xG underperformance on the attacking side of the ball is something that could come back to bite this team, as of the top five scorers this season, four are significantly underperforming their xG. Ester Gonzalez may no longer be with the team, but she was close to hitting her expected numbers with six goals from an xG of 7.85. Sam Kerr has four goals from 9.58 xG, Rose Lavelle has four from 6.26 xG, Jordynn Dudley has four from 5.76 xG, and then there's Shaw.

Date: Saturday, Oct. 17 | Time: 12:30 p.m. ETLocation: Audi Field -- Washington, D.C.TV: CBS Odds: Washington Spirit +160; Draw +230; NJ/NJ Gotham +155

While it was surprising not to see Shaw in the most recent United States women's national team camp facing Spain, Emma Hayes made the point that she wanted Shaw to play consistent minutes with the U-23s rather than come off the bench, given the national team's depth on the wings. That's understandable, but Gotham need Shaw to continue performing at this level, or an attack that is already underperforming will crater on the season.

Luckily, there isn't a position that Shaw can't get a shot off from or an area of the pitch that she can't score from. Be it cutting inside from the wing, popping up with a header or just taking a rip from outside the box, she's got you covered. And with so many other stars on this Gotham team, other NWSL sides can't focus on taking away Shaw because then someone else will make them pay. Despite the inefficiency in attack, the club's defense makes this a strong side, even if it's no longer the true super team Gotham were trying to build in New Jersey. That may even be for the best, as to win the NWSL championship, sides have to be well-rounded and have multiple avenues to create danger, which Gotham have. That could be enough to win a championship, but if a team can limit Shaw's shots, there are fair questions about what Gotham will do next. Facing the third-placed Washington Spirit will be a good test in that regard, but they're more of a test of Gotham's defense than they are of Gotham's attack. But to win a championship, they'll likely need to go through the Spirit anyway, and with the final set for Audi Field, this is good preparation, making Saturday's clash must-watch TV.

#1443. How good can Barcelona be? After an 11th straight win to start the season, is something historic brewing?

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

If not listed, please contact your TV provider.

It's 11 matches, 11 wins as Hansi Flick's Barcelona continue their perfect start to the season by defeating Real Betis 5-2. Who else but Lamine Yamal opened the scoring? In his age-19 season, he's taking a step forward despite already being a global superstar, which is scary for the rest of the world. Flick's men have dispatched every team in their path, and even that level of dominance doesn't do justice to what Barcelona have done this season. Barcelona executed their summer transfers to perfection, and while it's too early to begin discussing a perfect season, it's clear that they have the best attack in the world, and it isn't particularly close. Raphinha missed their last three matches due to injury, and the club didn't miss his 14 goals in all competitions, winning each match by three goals. It was slightly tougher facing Manuel Pellegrini's Real Betis, but if a team has to score four goals to have a chance of beating Barcelona this season, it brings an immense amount of pressure. Betis are off to an excellent start to the season with six wins in nine LaLiga matches, but they've scored 23 fewer goals than Barcelona. Yes, you read that correctly: 23 fewer goals. In fact, no LaLiga team has come within 20 goals of matching Barcelona's 39 goals in nine games.

While they've gotten off to an impressive start to the season, it's also worth looking at how they've done it.

Over the summer, Barcelona added Anthony Gordon, Karim Adeyemi, Gabriel Jesus and Rodri as they've finally gotten to a point where they can spend money again. Not only have the new attackers added speed in transition, but Rodri has also marshaled the defense so the squad is caught out in transition less often. Those new signings have also accounted for seven LaLiga goals between them and have also given Flick serious depth to rotate while also competing on multiple fronts. While it's true Barcelona are still conceding goals with Rodri in midfield due to their open play style, only five teams have conceded fewer goals than Barcelona, they don't need to have a stout defense, just one that's good enough to let their historic attack go to work. Keeping things in line is what will help this team contend for a Champions League crown, which is still the goal.

Weirdly, last season was a down season for Lamine Yamal as he missed time with injuries and was "only" involved in 27 LaLiga goals. Now he has scored eight and assisted six more in only nine games played as he's reaching yet another level. There are too many superlatives to describe what he's doing on the pitch, but his dribbling, vision and finishing are off the charts.

In Champions League play, he took a Galatasaray defender for a ride along the sideline before setting up Anthony Gordon for a goal, and with how much talent is around him, Barcelona can maximize what Yamal does well.

Lamine Yamal that is SENSATIONAL 🔥Barcelona's superstar provides two incredible assists for Fermín López and Anthony Gordon as Barcelona complete the comeback 💫Watch the #UCL on @ParamountPlus 📺 pic.twitter.com/QiOU23XtTF

Yamal is already a Ballon d'Or contender, and the fact that he's still improving his game means the rest of the world will need to look out. He's already doing things that haven't been done by anyone who isn't named Lionel Messi, and we're only scratching the surface of this potential. Sometimes players who break out at such a young age can plateau and stay at the same level for years, but when you're seeing clear improvement from him in a juggernaut of a team, anything could happen here. Even a perfect season -- it's too early to talk about one yet, but it could happen.

Even though he hasn't played in three games, it wouldn't be right to talk about Barcelona without mentioning the exploits of Raphinha and his position change. Flick has pushed him centrally to be a striker, and it has worked to perfection. He's leading the European Golden Shoe rankings for the top scorer across Europe's leagues, and you'd think that he has been a striker for his entire career. It's impressive management from Flick, and with Gabriel Jesus as the backup, the squad isn't missing a step even without their star man in the middle. It helps keep up the attacking pressure, and with a Clasico against Real Madrid looming next week, Barcelona can turn all of this into a commanding lead in LaLiga.

#1441. 'Keep your eyes on the road': Diogo Jota's new wife's text message to husband revealed in her heartbreaking account of how tragic Liverpool star's Lamborghini crash death unfolded

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Published: 11:59 BST, 22 October 2026 | Updated: 18:25 BST, 22 October 2026

Diogo Jota's widow Rute Cardoso has revealed she told him 'keep your eyes on the road' in her final message to the Liverpool star, as she gave a heartbreaking account of the night of his death in a new book.

In a biography about the late Liverpool forward, Cardoso explained she had exchanged messages with Jota shortly after he had left for the Spanish city Santander with his brother Andre Silva.

Jota had planned to travel to the city to drop off a Lamborghini he had rented shortly after the couple's wedding, before taking a ferry back to the UK for pre-season.

Cardoso revealed she had told Jota 'keep your eyes on the road' after he had responded to her message following his departure.

'It was a surprise,' Cardoso said in an extract of the book Diogo Jota: Forever In Our Hearts, shared by the Liverpool Echo, regarding his decision to rent a Ferrari then a Lamborghini in the summer of 2025.

'Unlike most players, who like fancy cars, jewellery and watches, Diogo was never into that sort of thing. He was happy with the cars we had.

Rute Cardoso has given a heartbreaking account of the night of her husband Diogo Jota's death in a new biography

Cardoso explains in the book that she grew increasing concerned after Jota and his brother Andre Silva, left, failed to respond to WhatsApp messages and had not arrived at their hotel

'So perhaps because he'd never been particularly into cars, he took that chance to try driving a Ferrari before our wedding. After that, he would also rent a Lamborghini, which he had to drop off in Santander.

'A few minutes after they'd left, I sent Diogo a message saying, "Safe journey". He replied with an emoji, and I told him to keep his eyes on the road.'

Jota and his younger brother died when the Lamborghini Huracan he was driving careered off a motorway in northern Spain.

Jota, 28, a father-of-three, was travelling back to Liverpool by road after he was advised by doctors not to fly following surgery, and crashed close to Spain's north-west border with Portugal.

He married his childhood sweetheart Cardoso at a church in Porto, Portugal, just 11 days before the devastating collision.

Cardoso explained how she had grown increasingly worried after several WhatsApp messages to Jota were left undelivered.

The first of which was Cardoso informing Jota she had a video to show him, having just received footage from their wedding day.

A subsequent message was also undelivered when she attempted to contact Andre.

Cardoso revealed she had requested to visit the location of the crash which killed Jota and his brother as she made her way to collect his belongings

She then rang the hotel the brothers had been due to stay at in Benavente to break up their journey. After being informed they had not arrived, Cardoso said she then phoned a hospital and the local police without success.

Cardoso then contacted her uncle Vitor, a lorry driver with good contacts in the Spanish transport police.

'Suddenly, my uncle asked me to put Nuno, my brother-in-law, on the line. At that time of night, in the silence, I could make out every word they were saying. I heard my uncle tell him: "The bodies are being taken away". I think he added "to the mortuary", but that's when my mind went blank,' Cardoso said in the book extract.

'Bodies? My brother-in-law froze. My sister took the phone from him and asked my uncle to speak to her. My main concern was to ring Diogo's father and tell him: "You must come here immediately". I rang André's girlfriend and said the same thing: "Paula, come over now".'

Cardoso revealed in the biography, written by former footballer Jose Manuel Delgado, that the family was visited by two psychologists to offer support.

Her uncle called again and passed Cardoso onto a Spanish police officer, who officially informed her of Jota's death.

Cardoso added in the family-authorised biography, which will support the LFC Foundation, that she requested to visit the scene of the accident when en route to mortuary to collect Jota and his brother’s belongings.

'I told the driver I wanted us to take the same route that they had taken,' Cardoso said. 'I imagined that, so many hours on, there would be no traces left, but I wanted to know where it had happened. But when we reached that cursed Kilometre 64, what was left of the car was still there.'

Cardoso had attempted to move closer to the scene of the accident but was stopped by a police officer.

Former Liverpool bosses Jurgen Klopp and Arne Slot are among those to have contributed to the biography about Jota

A funeral was held for both Jota and Silva two days after their devastating deaths in a chapel in Gondomar, a city that overlooks Porto, where both brothers played during their academy careers.

Following an investigation, a Spanish court ruled earlier this year that the crash was due to the Lamborghini suffering a blown-out tyre while overtaking a car, before bursting into flames upon hitting a barrier in the middle of the carriageway.

The High Court of Justice in the province of Zamora, north-west Spain, has concluded that no criminal action was necessary regarding the deaths of both Jota and Andre, following the review of the entire evidence.

Jota turned out 123 times for Liverpool, scoring 47 goals, and played a significant role in their 2024-25 Premier League title-winning season.

Former Liverpool bosses Arne Slot and Jurgen Klopp are among those to have contributed to the biography, along with former team-mates.

'This book began as an opportunity to help preserve Diogo’s memory at a moment of profound sadness. My commitment throughout was to tell his story with care, honesty and respect for Diogo, André, and the family and friends who loved them,' said author Delgado.

'What moved me most was the generosity of those closest to them. Family members, teammates, coaches and friends shared their memories to help future generations understand the person Diogo was, and their love for both brothers can be felt on every page.

'While researching the book, I came to understand the unique bond between Diogo and Liverpool FC. Everywhere I went, people spoke not only of his talent, but also his humility, commitment and character.

'My hope is that Forever in our Hearts serves as a fitting tribute to Diogo’s life and legacy, and ensures his story continues to inspire others for years to come.'

The Anfield faithful continuously paid their respects to Jota last season, singing his name in the 20th minute of every match, while the club also retired his No20 shirt.

A permanent memorial to Jota and Andre was installed at Anfield in July.
