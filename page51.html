#2659. Valencia vs Villarreal - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Matt Law | 22 Oct 2026 20:57, Last updated: 22 Oct 2026 20:59

Both Valencia and Villarreal will be aiming to return to winning ways in Spain's top flight when they lock horns on Saturday afternoon.

Los Che will enter the contest off the back of a goalless draw with Athletic Bilbao, while Villarreal played out a goalless draw with Elche in their last fixture.

Valencia have had a difficult start to the 2026-27 campaign, with just six points from their nine matches leaving them down in 19th spot in the La Liga table.

Los Che have won one, drawn three and lost five of their nine league games this term, and they have picked up two points from their last three games, drawing with Racing de Santander and Athletic in their last two.

Valencia's only victory of the campaign came away to Alaves in the middle of September, with Javier Aguirre still waiting for his first win since arriving as head coach.

The six-time Spanish champions have not actually overcome Villarreal since January 2024, with the Yellow Submarine unbeaten in the last five fixtures between the two sides.

Valencia suffered defeats to Villarreal in both league matches last season, losing 2-0 at Mestalla in October 2025 before the reverse fixture in February of this year ended 2-1 to the Yellow Submarine.

Villarreal will enter this match off the back of a 4-3 defeat to Liverpool in the Champions League; the La Liga outfit actually led 2-0 at Anfield, but Andoni Iraola's side fought back to secure all three points.

Inigo Perez's side drew 0-0 with Elche in the league last time out, with that result following a 1-0 reverse to Real Madrid in their first game after the September/October international break.

The Yellow Submarine have a record of two wins, three draws and four defeats from their nine league matches this season, which has left them in 12th spot in the table.

Villarreal are already seven points behind fifth-placed Real Sociedad, which will be a concern at this stage of the campaign, with the team desperately needing to put together a consistent run of results.

Valencia will be without the services of Umar Sadiq, Sergi Canos and Diego Lopez through injury, while Dimitri Foulquier and Copete are regarded as fitness doubts at this stage.

Hugo Duro started through the middle against Athletic last time out, but Arnaut Danjuma could be moved forward one to feature as the false nine this weekend.

Such a change could potentially see Luis Rioja introduced into the starting team here.

As for Villarreal, Nicolas Pepe is not expected to be back on the field until the end of the month, having recently undergone an eye operation.

Carlos Romero and Nathan Saliba are both viewed as fitness doubts, but Tajon Buchanan is available again, having recovered from a hamstring injury.

There will be changes to the side that took to the field for the first whistle against Liverpool, with Juan Foyth and Ayoze Perez set to be among those introduced into the XI.

#2652. CM: Why Milan’s win over Atalanta showed the best and worst of Amorim’s management

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

AC Milan managed to survive a late comeback attempt from Atalanta to beat them at San Siro last night, but there were some concerning signs.

Calciomercato.com write that the alarm on Atalanta’s bedside table may have gone off too late, but it must be said that Milan also helped them to get back into the game, one that seemed to be beyond them with 65 minutes gone.

Amorim gave the Milan fans a beautiful and exciting performance for an hour, with a three goal advantage built up over their opponents. Then, he completely mismanaged the substitutions, which put the result in doubt.

Omari Hutchinson for example is still tactically lagging behind for matches of this level despite his excellent performance in Salzburg. Pervis Estupinan made more mistakes in terms of both his positioning and his build-up play.  Ardon Jashari did not bring the required control either.

Let’s start with some positives. What immediately threw Atalanta’s game plan into disarray was Amorim’s tactical set-up, which deprived their opponents of being able to keep tabs on the key players.

Goncalo Ramos was constantly swapping positions with Christian Pulisic, Luka Modric alternated between pushing up and dropping deep, while Adrien Rabiot was all over the pitch again. The defensive line also alternated between a three and a four.

The former Sporting CP manager’s ability to rekindle struggling players like Yunus Musah and Samuel Chukwueze is evident to all, as is his ability to keep everyone in the squad attentive with rotation from game to game.

Another constant in Amorim’s footballing philosophy is to always dare to make substitutions. The 65th to 75th minute seems the time for changes in his mind. Against Torino, Juventus and Lazio they went well. Last night, not so much.

With the score at 3-0, Amorim brought on Estupinan, Jashari and Ruben Loftus-Cheek. The result? Atalanta had enormous space to attack, which, as the minutes ticked by, deprived Milan of confidence, causing their fans to experience 20 minutes of unjustified apnea.

A partial explanation certainly comes from a schedule that sees the team play every three days: on Thursday, Milan will play away in Bournemouth. The next step for Amorim is to find the balance between courageous management and a changes that bring freshness.

#2650. Samsunspor vs Amed SK - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Joshua Cole | 22 Oct 2026 21:08

Samsunspor will look to end their losing run when they host Amed SK in the Turkish Super Lig on Saturday at Samsun Yeni 19 Mayis Stadium.

Nenad Bjelica's side have suffered six consecutive league defeats and are in danger of becoming regulars in the bottom three this season, while their newly promoted visitors arrive with considerably greater confidence after winning four of their last six league matches and establishing themselves among the top sides in the division.

Samsunspor head into this fixture after losing 4-1 away to Kasimpasa, extending a difficult run that has left them 17th in the table after eight league matches, having collected just four points while scoring seven goals and conceding 19.

They have also suffered heavy defeats against Trabzonspor and Corum Belediyespor, while their average of 2.4 goals conceded per game leaves them with the second-worst defensive record in the league, better only than bottom-placed Eyupspor.

Bjelica therefore needs a more disciplined performance from his side, although he can take some encouragement from them ending their two-match scoring drought in his first game in charge, and he will hope they can build on that at home against one of the division's strongest sides so far this season.

However, Samsunspor's home results offer little encouragement, as they have lost their last three matches on their own turf and are without a win in four, scoring just once across their last three home games, with three of their four home goals this season coming in their opening 3-3 draw against Goztepe.

The Red Lightnings have conceded 13 goals at home, more than any other side in the league, while their solitary point at Samsun Yeni 19 Mayis Stadium is matched only by Caykur Rizespor.

This will be the first top-flight meeting between Samsunspor and Amed, with their four previous encounters taking place in the third division, where Samsunspor won twice, lost once and drew the other meeting.

Amed, meanwhile, have made an impressive start to life in the top flight, emerging as one of the division's most productive attacking sides with 18 goals in eight league matches, a tally bettered only by Fenerbahce's 22, and they entered the weekend second in the standings behind Galatasaray.

Besnik Hasi's team are on a six-match unbeaten run, comprising four wins and two draws, with that sequence including a 2-1 victory over Trabzonspor, a 3-2 win against Besiktas, a 5-0 triumph over Istanbul Basaksehir and a 1-0 success against Konyaspor in their most recent outing.

The Diyarbakir side have lost just one of their eight league matches, winning five, and sit second, two points behind reigning champions Galatasaray and one ahead of third-placed Fenerbahce.

However, there is a clear contrast between Amed's home and away performances, with 15 of their 17 points coming on home soil and just two collected from three away matches.

They lost 2-0 to Kocaelispor before recording consecutive 2-2 draws against Genclerbirligi and Kasimpasa, leaving them still searching for their first away victory of the season.

Samsunspor have had concerns over Celil Yuksel and Fatih Kaya, while Kouadou Assoumou is also doubtful with a muscle problem.

Bjelica has recently used Elliot Watt and Yalcin Kayan in central midfield, with Tanguy Coulibaly and Emre Kilinc among the options to support the attack.

Amed have also had fitness concerns involving Lumbardh Dellova and Dilhan Demir, while Yira Sor and Muhammed Hasan Yildirim have been among the players whose availability requires monitoring.

Ermal Krasniqi ranks third in the Super Lig for big chances created, with six, and will hope to add to that tally, while Gift Orban will be looking to maintain his scoring form after netting seven goals in eight league matches this season.

Lafont; Krasniqi, Yesil, Bates, Meras; Raveloson, Soyalp; Lutin, Saba, Ballet; Orban

Oviedo si è fermata per Lionel Messi. La presenza dell'argentino alla cerimonia del Premio Principessa delle Asturie ha generato un'attesa eccezionale, con tifosi radunati fin dalle prime ore davanti all'Hotel de la Reconquista e grande attenzione mediatica. Messi è stato premiato per "la sua eccezionale carriera sportiva, il suo talento straordinario e il suo costante impegno benefico" a favore dei bambini più svantaggiati. Anche il ct della Spagna Luis de la Fuente, presente al ricevimento reale, ha reso omaggio all'argentino: "L'eredità che Messi sta lasciando nello sport e nel calcio è eccezionale. L'entusiasmo aveva accompagnato Messi fin dal suo arrivo nelle Asturie giovedì mattina. Dopo il ricevimento reale e la cerimonia, per Messi è già tempo di tornare negli Stati Uniti: gli impegni con l'Inter Miami lo attendono immediatamente, con la partita di MLS contro i New York Red Bulls in programma sabato.

#2643. Amorim racking up firsts: All the key stats from AC Milan 3-2 Atalanta

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Ruben Amorim’s positive start to life as AC Milan head coach continued with a 3-2 win over Atalanta at San Siro on Sunday.

Milan were in a very commanding position with an hour on the clock. Goncalo Ramos converted a penalty, Adrien Rabiot netted his fourth of the season and Mario Gila scored his first goal for the club to give Ruben Amorim’s side a three-goal lead.

However, they nearly let it slip in the final 25 minutes. Gianluca Gaetano got Atalanta back into it and Nikola Krstovic made it a one-goal game with not long to go, but the Rossoneri hung on for their fourth win in seven Serie A games.

Milan recorded more possession in the game, but their 56% could have been higher if it weren’t for the very passive approach taken in the final 20-25 minutes.

The Rossoneri did generate comfortably more Expected Goals, with 2.41 to Atalanta’s 1.65. However, a large amount of that will have been from the two penalties, one of which Goncalo Ramos missed.

Adrien Rabiot has been involved in a goal for four matches in a row for the first time in his career in the ‘top five’ European leagues. The last midfielder to achieve this with Milan in Serie A was Hakan Çalhanoglu in December 2020.

Ruben Amorim (4W, 3D) has become the third coach in Milan history to remain unbeaten in each of his first seven matches in Serie A, after Hector Puricelli in 1955 (3W, 4D) and Luis Carniglia in 1963 (4W, 3D).

It was the first time that Amorim has beaten Atalanta as a head coach. His four previous meetings with them all came with Sporting CP in the Europa League, yielding two draws and two defeats.

Atalanta had gotten the better of Milan of late before last night, as they were unbeaten in the last six Serie A matches between the two teams.

The win for Milan was their first against Atalanta in the league since February 2023. The Orobici during that period had outscored the Rossoneri with 11 goals for and seven against.

The Orobici had been looking to reverse a three-match away winless streak. The last time they earned a win in a Serie A away game was May 10 against Milan.

Maurizio Sarri has now faced Milan on 22 occasions as a head coach in his career, recording eight wins, six draws and nine defeats.
