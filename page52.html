#2638. L'ex Bertotto: "Mi aspettavo un upgrade di Runjaic, con il Lecce ho visto un Udinese in balia"

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Nell'Udinese dal 1993 al 2006, Valerio Bertotto è stato intervistato da TuttoUdinese per parlare dell'attualità del club friulano. Queste le sue parole: "L'ultima gara dell'Udinese mi ha lasciato un po' l'amaro in bocca, perché ho visto una squadra in grave difficoltà e con poca attitudine nel voler cercare il risultato. Una squadra completamente in balia del Lecce, che ha meritato ampiamente la vittoria. È un peccato perché sicuramente l'Udinese, viste le capacità e le potenzialità che ha, non dovrebbe stare nella posizione che attualmente occupa. Quindi l'augurio è che si possa riprendere".

Si è rotto qualcosa tra Runjaic e la squadra? "È chiaro che tutti quanti si aspettavano, visti i buoni risultati che ha ottenuto, di fare un ulteriore upgrade e di salire di livello. Questo assolutamente sì, mi sembra naturale e ovvio, ed è un peccato che non sia successo. Chiaramente questo poi genera malcontento".

Cosa può dare Alaba? "Ovviamente stiamo parlando di un calciatore e di un uomo che può assolutamente dare un grande contributo tecnico e morale all'interno del gruppo, indicando la retta via e gli atteggiamenti giusti che ci vogliono per affrontare un campionato di Serie A. È importante anche per aiutare a crescere i tanti giovani presenti in rosa: questo è sinonimo di garanzia. Ho letto che ha avuto un problemino fisico e spero di rivederlo presto in campo sano, perché in questo momento non lo è ancora. Può essere davvero un valore aggiunto".

Qualcuno la sta deludendo o è colpa degli infortuni? "Non è giusto puntare il dito su nessuno singolarmente quando le cose non vanno bene a livello generale. Non c'è un singolo che fa la differenza, sia in positivo sia in negativo, ma di solito è la squadra che aiuta a fare la differenza. La sfida mentale da parte di tutti i calciatori è quella di spingere forte per risolvere prestissimo questa situazione. Di certo anche il fattore infortuni non aiuta: bisogna sistemare questo aspetto perché, in qualunque squadra, non avere determinati giocatori fa molta differenza ed è una delle cose che più incide sul livello e sul tasso tecnico".

#2635. Estrela Amadora vs Casa Pia - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Joshua Ojele | 22 Oct 2026 21:18, Last updated: 23 Oct 2026 09:30

Having both secured victories in the Taca de Portugal last time out, Estrela Amadora and Casa Pia return to action in the Primeira Liga when they square off at the Estadio Joao Gomes on Saturday afternoon.

While Estrela picked up victories in both home and away meetings between the two sides last season, Os Gansos secured a 2-1 win when they squared off in a pre-season clash behind closed doors on July 18.

Estrela Amadora avoided an early upset in their opening game of the 2026-27 Taca de Portugal campaign last Saturday when they secured a 2-1 victory over a dogged Fazendense side at the Estadio Dr. Jose Sousa Gomes.

The Campeonato de Portugal outfit held their own to keep the visitors at bay in the first half, but Tom Moustier netted on either side of Ivanilson’s 79th-minute equaliser to send the Tricolores through to the fourth round.

This was a timely lift for Estrela Amadora, who now turn their focus to the Primeira Liga, where they suffered back-to-back defeats against Academico Viseu and Arouca in their most recent two outings, having gone unbeaten in the opening six matches.

After netting 14 goals in the first six games, Pepa’s men failed to find the back of the net in their last two league outings, an area needing improvement sooner rather than later if they are to arrest their recent slump in form.

Estrela Amadora have picked up two wins and four draws from their eight league matches this season to collect 10 points and sit 10th in the Primeira Liga standings, but they could leapfrog Alverca and Braga into eighth place with a win on Saturday.

Like this weekend’s hosts, Casa Pia kicked off their Taca de Portugal campaign on a winning note last Sunday when they edged out Campeonato de Portugal Alpendorada 1-0 in their third-round clash.

Abdu Dafe’s sending-off after the break saw Os Gansos play the final seven minutes one man down at the Estadio Municipal de Alpendorada, but Sebastian Perez’s 73rd-minute strike proved decisive for the visitors, who held on to see out the result.

Head coach Filipe Coelho will hope the cup victory can serve as a catalyst for a much-needed turnaround in the league, where Os Gansos were sent crashing back to earth in a 1-0 home defeat at the hands of Santa Clara on October 10.

Having edged out Estoril Praia 2-1 on September 20 to snap their six-game winless run and get off the mark in the league, Casa Pia were left empty-handed at the Estadio Municipal de Rio Maior after Joao Vasconcelos netted in the 92nd minute to hand Santa Clara a last-gasp victory.

Five months after edging out Torreense in the relegation playoffs to retain their top-flight status, early signs point to another tough league campaign for Casa Pia, who sit bottom but one in the Primeira Liga table with four points from the first 24 available, but a win this weekend could see them rise level with 13th-placed Famalicao.

Estrela Amadora will have to cope without the services of summer signing Jeremy Arevalo, who is yet to feature this season after sustaining a back injury in July.

Abraham Marcus, Leandro Antonetti and Ianis Stoica have each netted four goals this season, and with a combined 16 goal contributions between the trio, we expect them to lead the hosts’ attack once again.

Meanwhile, Casa Pia will welcome back defender David Sousa, who was sent off against Santa Clara but served his one-game ban in the Taca de Portugal.

On the injury front, Portuguese goalkeeper Andre Gomes has missed the last four matches through a muscle problem, meaning Ivan Mandic should start between the sticks once again.

French forward Jeremy Livolant, who racked up eight assists and three goals last term, is yet to make an appearance this season due to injury and the 28-year-old remains out of contention for Os Gansos.

Estrela Amadora possible starting lineup:

Linck; Scholze, Patrick, Lekovic, Soares; Serra, Doue, Zvonarek; Marcus, Antonetti, Stoica

Mandic; Rosas, Sousa, Kaly, Geraldes; Ofori, Clua; Pauwels, Rego, Salah; Araujo

Buoyed by their cup victories, Estrela Amadora and Casa Pia will return to Primeira Liga action with some level of confidence as they look to find their feet in the league. Both sides are evenly matched on paper, making it a tough one to call, but we predict Estrela will find their scoring boots here and heap more misery on the struggling visitors.

#2631. GdS: Summer doubts, present certainty – Maignan earns Milan more points with huge save

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Every player must contribute to a winning cause, but what Mike Maignan did on Sunday night saved the result against Atalanta.

La Gazzetta dello Sport jokingly describe Maignan as ‘a goalkeeper with balls’ in a nod to what was his finest save on a night that started so well for Milan, and then begun to take a rapid descent in the 65th minute.

The moment when Milan almost threw away their three-goal lead came in the 83rd minute, when Zappacosta entered the heart of the Rossoneri penalty area without much effort – something we’d seen several times in the preceding minutes – and was 1v1 with Maignan.

The Frenchman closed the gap, coming towards him, and when the Nerazzurri winger shot, he positioned himself in the only way possible: trying to spread his legs and arms as wide as possible to block his opponent’s field of vision.

Zappacosta shot, and Mike blocked (with which body part we’ve already explained) what many thought was a certain goal. From there the Rossoneri just about saw the game out, but had they conceded who knows what result we could be talking about.

When Maignan cleared the ball, many celebrated as if it were a goal. Mike, however, was on the ground in pain, but he knew full well that he had saved Milan from a result that is difficult to explain after an overwhelming first hour.

These are points that the former Lille man brings to the table along with those who appear on the scoresheet, and ultimately they make a beautiful story when you think back to all the summer question marks.

Maignan left for the World Cup with his head full of worries, not knowing what kind of Milan he would find upon his return. Perhaps he also wondered if he himself was truly happy to come back after such a difficult year, we’ll never know for sure.

His contract extension until 2031 signed months ago more than adequately protected the club, but Mike is a proud and ambitious man. He wants to win trophies and he wants to play in the Champions League. Milan haven’t done either enough.

To the summer doubts about the Diavolo was then added the profound disappointment of the World Cup, with the sad parade of the third-placed play-off against England in which he found himself buried under the weight and shame of six goals.

Then came the controversy surrounding Franco Baresi’s funeral (the Rossoneri fans would have liked to see him present as captain). Finally, there was the controversy surrounding the extra holidays requested and granted by Amorim to him and Adrien Rabiot.

When Maignan returned to Milanello, the question marks vanished like a cloud of smoke. He made himself available to Amorim, met the new goalkeeping coach (he was very close to Claudio Filippi, brought in by Allegri), and began working without asking too many questions.

The save on Zappacosta wasn’t just worth two points. For this young and under-construction Milan, allowing three consecutive goals to throw the game away would have caused a storm, but Mike made sure no umbrella is needed.

#2629. Atletico Madrid vs Deportivo La Coruna - prediction, team news, lineups

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

By Matt Law | 22 Oct 2026 21:26, Last updated: 22 Oct 2026 21:29

Atletico Madrid will be aiming to make it six straight wins in Spain's top flight when they continue their campaign at home to Deportivo La Coruna on Saturday evening.

Diego Simeone's side will enter the match off the back of a 1-1 draw with Stuttgart in the Champions League, while Deportivo drew 1-1 with Levante in their last league match.

Atletico had European commitments in Germany ahead of this match, playing out a 1-1 draw with Stuttgart in the league phase of the Champions League, which made it four points from their three matches in the competition.

Simeone's team are flying in Spain's top flight, meanwhile, picking up 22 points from their nine matches to sit second in the La Liga table, one point ahead of third-placed Real Madrid and five behind the leaders Barcelona.

The Red and Whites suffered a shock 3-0 loss to Athletic Bilbao in La Liga at the start of September but have since claimed maximum points from their games against Real Sociedad, Osasuna, Real Madrid, Alaves and Espanyol.

Atletico could close the gap on leaders Barcelona this weekend, with the champions facing Real Madrid on Sunday night, and Simeone's team need to remain in striking distance.

The Red and Whites have been victorious in five of their last six matches against Deportivo and have not actually been beaten by Saturday's opponents since November 2009.

Deportivo's last away league success over Atletico came in November 1999, demonstrating the size of the away team's task in Saturday's contest.

The visitors drew 1-1 with Levante last time out, which made it five matches without a victory in Spain's top flight, and they currently sit in ninth position in the table on 11 points.

The Blue and Whites finished second in the Segunda Division last season, and their start to this campaign has been impressive, winning two, drawing five and losing two of their nine fixtures.

Antonio Hidalgo's side are only three points behind sixth-placed Sevilla, but recent injuries in the final third of the field have cost them of late.

Deportivo will know that they are up against it on Saturday, but Atletico's European exertions could hand the visitors a boost.

Atletico will be without the services of Koke, who has a hamstring injury, while an adductor strain will keep Cristian Romero out until at least the start of November.

Lee Kang-in has overcome a thigh issue, though, and should feature in the starting team.

Julian Alvarez scored his first goal of the season against Stuttgart in the Champions League last time out, and the Argentina international could continue through the middle on Saturday.

As for Deportivo, Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang, Zakaria Eddahchouri, Marc Casado and Bright Ede remain on the sidelines, while Noe Carrillo is a doubt due to a knock.

The squad will be boosted by the return of Lorenzo Amatucci, though, following a suspension.

Aubameyang's absence is a major one given his five goals for the team this season; Bil Nsongo is likely to continue through the middle for the Blue and Whites.

Atletico Madrid possible starting lineup:

Deportivo La Coruna possible starting lineup:

Deportivo's attacking absences are harming them at the moment, and we are expecting an in-form Atletico to collect all three points on Saturday without any major drama.

#2623. “Felt that I need a change” – Krösche issues statement as German sources confirm Milan links

📅 26 Oct 2026 | other | Source

Confirmations are beginning to arrive from Germany regarding Markus Krösche’s imminent move to AC Milan after he left Eintracht Frankfurt.

It looks like being a case of second time lucky for Milan after they tried to hire Krösche in the summer but were knocked back by Eintracht, who were not happy about letting him go. Matteo Moretto and Fabrizio Romano are both reporting that talks are ‘advanced’ with the German director.

Read SempreMilan ad-free and get access to exclusive news. Click here for a free trial!

The reason that a move is possible mid-season is because Krösche has had his resignation accepted by the Bundesliga side. A statement came from him too as part of the announcement.

“The past few years at Eintracht Frankfurt have been an extraordinary, intense and unforgettable period for me. Together, we have celebrated great successes and helped the club develop both on the pitch and financially. Winning the Europa League and sharing so many special moments with our fans will always mean a great deal to me. I am extremely grateful for those experiences.

“Over the past few months, I have spent a lot of time reflecting on my personal circumstances and professional future. After more than five very successful but also very demanding years at this wonderful club, I have increasingly felt that I need a change – and that a new impetus might also benefit Eintracht.

“When you reach that point, you inevitably start to lose some of the drive and vigor required for such a demanding role. I spoke openly with Mathias Beck about this because I expect myself to approach a position of this responsibility with complete conviction at all times.

“That is why I asked Mathias Beck to release me from my duties with immediate effect. This gives both Eintracht and me enough time to prepare for the future. I also wanted the situation to be resolved before the general meeting because it is important to me to have the opportunity to say goodbye in person.

“I’m very grateful to Mathias Beck and the supervisory board for the open and fair discussions conducted in a spirit of mutual trust, as well as for the respectful way in which they handled my request.

“I would also like to extend my sincere thanks to my fellow board members. We enjoyed a very open, trusting and successful working relationship.

“My special thanks go to all the staff, coaches, players and, of course, our incredible fans. Eintracht Frankfurt will always be a very special club to me, and I wish everyone involved nothing but the best for the future.”

Bild report that Milan want Krösche to join and play a key role in their sporting department. The 46-year-old is set to join as the so-called Head of Football by title.

The Rossoneri, currently fourth in Serie A, believe the German is capable of transforming them in a similarly successful way as he did five years ago at Eintracht Frankfurt.

Talks between Krösche and Milan have progressed significantly in recent days and an agreement ‘could be finalised by the end of this week’.

This story isn’t new: Milan had already courted Krösche last summer . The then-sporting director of Eintracht Frankfurt negotiated with the Diavolo for several days. He was to be given full responsibility for the squad and staff, and the financial package at the time was €40m over four years.

Kicker (via MilanNews) add the following: “Eintracht Frankfurt would also be ‘happy’ for one specific reason. Krosche’s contract, renewed in 2024 until June 30, 2028, is currently still valid.

“This means that Eintracht Frankfurt can still receive compensation for Krösche’s transfer if the director reaches an agreement with another club during that period.”
